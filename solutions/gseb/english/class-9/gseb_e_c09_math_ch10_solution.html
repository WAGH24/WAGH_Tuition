Solved ExamplesHeron’s formula

📋 Given
Find the area of a triangle whose two sides are 8 cm and 11 cm and whose perimeter is 32 cm.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Find the third side, then use Heron’s formula \(\Delta=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}\).
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Third side \(=32-8-11=13\) cm.
  2. Semiperimeter \(s=32/2=16\) cm.
  3. \(\Delta=\sqrt{16(16-8)(16-11)(16-13)}\).
  4. \(=\sqrt{16\cdot8\cdot5\cdot3}=8\sqrt{30}\text{ cm}^2\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{8\sqrt{30}\text{ cm}^2}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{8\sqrt{30}\text{ સેમી}^2}\).
📋 Given
Sides are \(120\text{ m},80\text{ m},50\text{ m}\). Fencing rate is ₹20 per metre and a 3 m gate is left unfenced.
🎯 To Find
(i) Area of the park.
(ii) Cost of fencing.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Heron’s formula: \(A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}\), where \(s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}\).
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Perimeter \(=120+80+50=250\text{ m}\).
  2. \(s=\frac{250}{2}=125\text{ m}\).
  3. \(s-a=5\), \(s-b=45\), \(s-c=75\).
  4. \(A=\sqrt{125\times5\times45\times75}\).
  5. \(A=375\sqrt{15}\text{ m}^2\).
  6. Length of wire \(=250-3=247\text{ m}\).
  7. Cost \(=247\times₹20=₹4940\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\text{Area}=375\sqrt{15}\text{ m}^2,\;\text{fencing cost}=₹4940}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ક્ષેત્રફળ \(375\sqrt{15} ext{ મીટર}^2\) અને વાડનો ખર્ચ ₹4940 છે.
📋 Given
The sides of a triangular plot are in the ratio \(3:5:7\), and its perimeter is 300 m. Find its area.

Complete the required proof or calculation.

Convert the ratio to actual sides, then apply Heron’s formula.

  1. Let sides be \(3x,5x,7x\). Then \(15x=300\Rightarrow x=20\).

  4. \(=1500\sqrt3\text{ m}^2\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{1500\sqrt3\text{ m}^2}\).

Exercise 10.1Area of triangles

📋 Given
A traffic signal board is an equilateral triangle with side \(a\). Find its area using Heron’s formula. If its perimeter is 180 cm, find its area.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
For an equilateral triangle, \(s=3a/2\); apply Heron’s formula.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(s=\frac{3a}{2}\).
  2. \(\Delta=\sqrt{\frac{3a}{2}\left(\frac a2\right)^3}=\frac{\sqrt3}{4}a^2\).
  3. If perimeter \(=180\) cm, then \(a=60\) cm.
  4. Area \(=\frac{\sqrt3}{4}(60)^2=900\sqrt3\text{ cm}^2\).
✅ Final Answer
General area \(\boxed{\frac{\sqrt3}{4}a^2}\); for perimeter 180 cm, \(\boxed{900\sqrt3\text{ cm}^2}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
સામાન્ય ક્ષેત્રફળ \(\frac{\sqrt3}{4}a^2\); 180 સેમી પરિમિતિ માટે \(900\sqrt3\text{ સેમી}^2\).
📋 Given
The triangular side walls of a flyover have sides 122 m, 22 m and 120 m. Advertisement earnings are ₹5000 per square metre per year. A company hired one wall for 3 months. How much rent did it pay?
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use Heron’s formula and prorate the yearly rate for 3 months.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(s=(122+22+120)/2=132\) m.
  2. Area \(=\sqrt{132(10)(110)(12)}\).
  3. \(=\sqrt{1742400}=1320\text{ m}^2\).
  4. Annual rent \(=1320\times5000=₹66,00,000\).
  5. For 3 months: \(₹66,00,000\times\frac3{12}=₹16,50,000\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{₹16,50,000}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{₹16,50,000}\).
