Solved ExamplesParallelograms and midpoint theorem

📋 Given
Show that each angle of a rectangle is a right angle.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
A rectangle is a parallelogram with one right angle; consecutive angles are supplementary and opposite angles are equal.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let one angle be \(90^\circ\).
  2. Each adjacent angle is supplementary to it, hence also \(90^\circ\).
  3. Opposite angles are equal.
  4. Therefore all four angles are \(90^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
Every angle of a rectangle is a right angle.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
લંબચોરસનો દરેક ખૂણો સમકોણ છે.
📋 Given
Show that the diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular to each other.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use diagonal bisection of a parallelogram and SSS congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let diagonals \(AC\) and \(BD\) of rhombus \(ABCD\) meet at \(O\).
  2. As a rhombus is a parallelogram, \(AO=OC\) and \(BO=OD\).
  3. In \(\triangle AOB\) and \(\triangle AOD\), \(AB=AD\), \(AO\) common, and \(BO=OD\); hence SSS congruent.
  4. Thus \(\angle BOA=\angle AOD\). They form a linear pair, so each is \(90^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AC\perp BD}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{AC\perp BD}\).
📋 Given
\(AB=AC\), \(AD\) bisects exterior angle \(PAC\), and \(CD\parallel AB\).
🎯 To Find
(i) Prove \(\angle DAC=\angle BCA\).
(ii) Prove \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use the isosceles-triangle angle property, the exterior-angle theorem, and the test that both pairs of opposite sides parallel imply a parallelogram.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Because \(AB=AC\), \(\angle ABC=\angle ACB\).
  2. Exterior angle \(\angle PAC=\angle ABC+\angle ACB=2\angle ACB\).
  3. Because \(AD\) bisects \(\angle PAC\), \(2\angle DAC=\angle PAC\).
  4. Therefore \(2\angle DAC=2\angle ACB\), so \(\angle DAC=\angle ACB=\angle BCA\).
  5. These are alternate interior angles for lines \(AD\) and \(BC\) with transversal \(AC\); hence \(AD\parallel BC\).
  6. Also \(AB\parallel CD\) is given.
  7. Both pairs of opposite sides of quadrilateral \(ABCD\) are parallel.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle DAC=\angle BCA}\) and \(\boxed{ABCD\text{ is a parallelogram}}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
આથી \(\angle DAC=\angle BCA\) અને \(ABCD\) સમાંતરબાજુ ચતુષ્કોણ છે.
📋 Given
Lines \(l\parallel m\), transversal \(p\), and the four interior-angle bisectors form quadrilateral \(ABCD\).
🎯 To Find
Prove that \(ABCD\) is a rectangle.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use alternate-angle equality, angle bisectors, and supplementary interior angles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Alternate interior angles made by \(l\parallel m\) are equal.
  2. Their halves are equal, so a pair of alternate angles of the bisector lines is equal; hence \(AB\parallel DC\).
  3. Similarly, \(BC\parallel AD\).
  4. Thus \(ABCD\) is a parallelogram.
  5. Two interior angles on the same side of the transversal have sum \(180^\circ\).
  6. The angle between their bisectors is \(\frac12\times180^\circ=90^\circ\).
  7. A parallelogram with one right angle is a rectangle.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{ABCD\text{ is a rectangle}}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(ABCD\) લંબચોરસ છે.
📋 Given
In parallelogram \(ABCD\), the internal angle bisectors meet successively at \(P,Q,R,S\).
🎯 To Find
Prove that \(PQRS\) is a rectangle.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Adjacent angles of a parallelogram are supplementary; opposite angles are equal.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle A+\angle D=180^\circ\).
  2. The angle between their bisectors is \(\frac12(\angle A+\angle D)=90^\circ\).
  3. Therefore one angle of \(PQRS\) is \(90^\circ\).
  4. The same reasoning gives all four angles of \(PQRS\) equal to \(90^\circ\).
  5. A quadrilateral with four right angles is a rectangle.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{PQRS\text{ is a rectangle}}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(PQRS\) લંબચોરસ છે.
📋 Given
\(D,E,F\) are the mid-points of \(AB,BC,CA\), respectively.
🎯 To Find
Prove that \(\triangle ADF\), \(\triangle BDE\), \(\triangle CEF\) and \(\triangle DEF\) are congruent.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use the midpoint theorem and SSS congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. By the midpoint theorem, \(DE=\frac12 AC\), \(EF=\frac12 AB\), and \(FD=\frac12 BC\).
  2. Also \(AD=DB=\frac12 AB\), \(BE=EC=\frac12 BC\), and \(CF=FA=\frac12 AC\).
  3. Each of the four small triangles therefore has side lengths \(\frac12 AB,\frac12 BC,\frac12 CA\).
  4. Hence all four triangles are congruent by SSS.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\triangle ADF\cong\triangle BDE\cong\triangle CEF\cong\triangle DEF}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ચારેય નાના ત્રિકોણ SSS નિયમથી સર્વાંગસમ છે.
📋 Given
Three parallel lines cut two transversals. If the intercepts between consecutive parallels on one transversal are equal, show that the corresponding intercepts on the other transversal are equal.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Draw a suitable auxiliary parallel and apply midpoint theorem/congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let the three parallel lines meet the first transversal at \(A,B,C\) with \(AB=BC\), and the second at \(P,Q,R\).
  2. Draw through \(B\) a line parallel to \(PR\) meeting the other parallels appropriately.
  3. The midpoint theorem/congruent triangles show that the corresponding two intercepts on the second transversal are equal.
  4. Hence \(PQ=QR\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{PQ=QR}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{PQ=QR}\).

Exercise 8.1Parallelogram and special quadrilaterals

📋 Given
If the diagonals of a parallelogram are equal, show that it is a rectangle.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Congruence of triangles using SSS and adjacent supplementary angles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let diagonals \(AC=BD\) in parallelogram \(ABCD\).
  2. In \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle BAD\), \(AB\) is common, \(BC=AD\), and \(AC=BD\).
  3. The triangles are congruent by SSS, so \(\angle ABC=\angle BAD\).
  4. Adjacent angles of a parallelogram are supplementary.
  5. Equal supplementary angles are each \(90^\circ\), so all angles are right angles.
✅ Final Answer
The parallelogram is a rectangle.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
સમાંતરબાજુ ચતુર્ભુજ લંબચોરસ છે.
📋 Given
Show that the diagonals of a square are equal and bisect each other at right angles.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
A square is both a rectangle and a rhombus.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. As a parallelogram, its diagonals bisect each other.
  2. As a rectangle, its diagonals are equal.
  3. As a rhombus, its diagonals are perpendicular.
  4. Therefore all three stated properties hold.
✅ Final Answer
The diagonals are equal, mutually bisecting, and perpendicular.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ચોરસના વિકર્ણો સમાન, પરસ્પર સમદ્વિભાજક અને લંબ છે.
📋 Given
Diagonal \(AC\) of parallelogram \(ABCD\) bisects \(\angle A\). Show that (i) it bisects \(\angle C\), and (ii) \(ABCD\) is a rhombus.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Parallel-line angle relations and congruence/equal opposite sides.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Given \(\angle DAC=\angle CAB\).
  2. Because \(AD\parallel BC\), \(\angle DAC=\angle BCA\). Because \(AB\parallel CD\), \(\angle CAB=\angle ACD\).
  3. Thus \(\angle BCA=\angle ACD\), so \(AC\) bisects \(\angle C\).
  4. In \(\triangle ABC\), \(\angle BAC=\angle BCA\), hence \(AB=BC\).
  5. A parallelogram with adjacent sides equal has all four sides equal; therefore it is a rhombus.
✅ Final Answer
\(AC\) bisects \(\angle C\), and \(ABCD\) is a rhombus.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
AC, ∠Cને દ્વિભાજિત કરે છે અને ABCD સમચતુર્ભુજ છે.
📋 Given
Diagonal \(AC\) of rectangle \(ABCD\) bisects \(\angle A\) and \(\angle C\). Show that (i) \(ABCD\) is a square, and (ii) diagonal \(BD\) bisects \(\angle B\) and \(\angle D\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Equal angles imply equal sides; a rectangle with adjacent sides equal is a square.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. In \(\triangle ABC\), \(\angle BAC=\angle ACB\), hence \(AB=BC\).
  2. A rectangle with adjacent sides equal has all four sides equal, so it is a square.
  3. In a square, triangles formed by diagonal \(BD\) have three corresponding equal sides.
  4. By SSS, corresponding angles at \(B\) and \(D\) are equal; hence \(BD\) bisects both angles.
✅ Final Answer
ABCD is a square and \(BD\) bisects \(\angle B\) and \(\angle D\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ABCD ચોરસ છે અને BD, ∠B તથા ∠Dને દ્વિભાજિત કરે છે.
📋 Given
In parallelogram \(ABCD\), points \(P,Q\) lie on diagonal \(BD\) such that \(DP=BQ\). Prove: (i) \(\triangle APD\cong\triangle CQB\); (ii) \(AP=CQ\); (iii) \(\triangle AQB\cong\triangle CPD\); (iv) \(AQ=CP\); (v) \(APCQ\) is a parallelogram.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SAS congruence using opposite sides and alternate angles, followed by opposite-side criterion.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. (i) \(AD=CB\), \(DP=BQ\), and \(\angle ADP=\angle CBQ\); hence SAS congruent.
  2. (ii) CPCT gives \(AP=CQ\).
  3. (iii) \(AB=CD\), \(BQ=DP\), and \(\angle ABQ=\angle CDP\); hence SAS congruent.
  4. (iv) CPCT gives \(AQ=CP\).
  5. (v) In quadrilateral \(APCQ\), both pairs of opposite sides are equal, so it is a parallelogram.
✅ Final Answer
All five statements are proved.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
પાંચેય નિવેદનો સાબિત થાય છે.
📋 Given
In parallelogram \(ABCD\), \(AP\) and \(CQ\) are perpendiculars from \(A\) and \(C\) to diagonal \(BD\). Prove that (i) \(\triangle APB\cong\triangle CQD\), and (ii) \(AP=CQ\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
AAS congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle APB=\angle CQD=90^\circ\).
  2. \(AB=CD\) as opposite sides of a parallelogram.
  3. \(\angle ABP=\angle CDQ\) because \(AB\parallel CD\).
  4. Therefore the right triangles are congruent by AAS.
  5. CPCT gives \(AP=CQ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\triangle APB\cong\triangle CQD\text{ and }AP=CQ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
બંને ત્રિકોણ સર્વાંગસમ અને AP=CQ.
📋 Given
\(ABCD\) is a trapezium with \(AB\parallel CD\) and \(AD=BC\). Prove: (i) \(\angle A=\angle B\); (ii) \(\angle C=\angle D\); (iii) \(\triangle ABC\cong\triangle BAD\); (iv) \(AC=BD\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Construct perpendiculars or use a translation to prove base angles of an isosceles trapezium, then SAS congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Drop perpendiculars from \(C,D\) to \(AB\). The resulting right triangles have equal hypotenuses \(AD=BC\) and equal perpendicular distances between parallels.
  2. By RHS, the right triangles are congruent, so \(\angle A=\angle B\).
  3. Supplementary interior angles then give \(\angle C=\angle D\).
  4. In \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle BAD\), \(AB\) common, \(BC=AD\), and included angles \(B=A\), so SAS congruent.
  5. Hence \(AC=BD\).
✅ Final Answer
All four properties of the isosceles trapezium are proved.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
સમદ્વિબાજુ સમલંબ ચતુર્ભુજના ચારેય ગુણધર્મો સાબિત થાય છે.

Exercise 8.2Midpoint theorem applications

📋 Given
\(P,Q,R,S\) are the midpoints of sides \(AB,BC,CD,DA\) of quadrilateral \(ABCD\). With diagonal \(AC\), show: (i) \(SR\parallel AC\) and \(SR=\frac12AC\); (ii) \(PQ=SR\); (iii) \(PQRS\) is a parallelogram.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Midpoint theorem in triangles \(ADC\) and \(ABC\).
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. In \(\triangle ADC\), \(S,R\) are midpoints, so \(SR\parallel AC\) and \(SR=\frac12AC\).
  2. In \(\triangle ABC\), \(P,Q\) are midpoints, so \(PQ\parallel AC\) and \(PQ=\frac12AC\).
  3. Therefore \(PQ=SR\) and \(PQ\parallel SR\).
  4. One pair of opposite sides equal and parallel implies \(PQRS\) is a parallelogram.
✅ Final Answer
All three statements are proved.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ત્રણેય નિવેદનો સાબિત થાય છે.
📋 Given
\(ABCD\) is a rhombus and \(P,Q,R,S\) are the midpoints of its sides. Show that \(PQRS\) is a rectangle.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Midpoint theorem and perpendicular diagonals of a rhombus.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Midpoint theorem gives \(PQ\parallel AC\) and \(QR\parallel BD\).
  2. Diagonals \(AC\perp BD\) in a rhombus.
  3. Therefore \(PQ\perp QR\).
  4. From the midpoint theorem, opposite sides of \(PQRS\) are parallel, so it is a parallelogram with one right angle.
  5. Hence it is a rectangle.
✅ Final Answer
PQRS is a rectangle.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
PQRS લંબચોરસ છે.
📋 Given
\(ABCD\) is a rectangle and \(P,Q,R,S\) are the midpoints of its sides. Show that \(PQRS\) is a rhombus.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Midpoint theorem and equal diagonals of a rectangle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(PQ=\frac12AC\), \(QR=\frac12BD\), and similarly for the other two sides.
  2. In a rectangle, \(AC=BD\).
  3. Therefore \(PQ=QR=RS=SP\).
  4. A quadrilateral with all four sides equal is a rhombus.
✅ Final Answer
PQRS is a rhombus.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
PQRS સમચતુર્ભુજ છે.
📋 Given
\(ABCD\) is a trapezium with \(AB\parallel DC\). \(BD\) is a diagonal and \(E\) is the midpoint of \(AD\). Through \(E\), draw a line parallel to \(AB\) meeting \(BC\) at \(F\). Show that \(F\) is the midpoint of \(BC\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Midpoint theorem applied through the diagonal.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let the line through \(E\) meet diagonal \(BD\) at \(G\).
  2. In \(\triangle DAB\), \(E\) is midpoint of \(DA\) and \(EG\parallel AB\), so \(G\) is midpoint of \(DB\).
  3. In \(\triangle DBC\), \(G\) is midpoint of \(DB\) and \(GF\parallel DC\) because \(AB\parallel DC\).
  4. By converse midpoint theorem, \(F\) is midpoint of \(BC\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{BF=FC}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{BF=FC}\).
📋 Given
In parallelogram \(ABCD\), \(E\) and \(F\) are the midpoints of \(AB\) and \(CD\). Show that the segments \(AF\) and \(EC\) trisect diagonal \(BD\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use similar triangles/parallel lines to establish equal three parts on the diagonal.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let \(AF\) and \(EC\) meet \(BD\) at \(P\) and \(Q\), respectively.
  2. Because \(AB\parallel CD\) and \(AE=EB=\frac12AB\), the relevant triangles formed with diagonal \(BD\) are similar.
  3. The similarity ratios give \(DP=PQ=QB\).
  4. Thus the two lines divide \(BD\) into three equal segments.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{DP=PQ=QB}\); hence \(AF\) and \(EC\) trisect \(BD\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
AF અને EC, BDને ત્રણ સમાન ભાગમાં વહેંચે છે.
📋 Given
In right triangle \(ABC\), \(\angle C=90^\circ\), \(M\) is the midpoint of hypotenuse \(AB\), and the line through \(M\) parallel to \(BC\) meets \(AC\) at \(D\). Show: (i) \(D\) is midpoint of \(AC\); (ii) \(MD\perp AC\); (iii) \(CM=MA=\frac12AB\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Midpoint theorem and properties of a rectangle/half-hypotenuse theorem.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. In \(\triangle ABC\), \(M\) is midpoint of \(AB\) and \(MD\parallel BC\); by converse midpoint theorem, \(D\) is midpoint of \(AC\).
  2. Since \(BC\perp AC\) and \(MD\parallel BC\), \(MD\perp AC\).
  3. Reflect/complete the rectangle or use congruent right triangles to obtain \(CM=MA\).
  4. As \(M\) is midpoint of \(AB\), \(MA=\frac12AB\). Hence \(CM=MA=\frac12AB\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AD=DC,\;MD\perp AC,\;CM=MA=\frac12AB}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
AD=DC, MD⊥AC અને CM=MA=AB/2.
