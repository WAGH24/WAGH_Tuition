Solved ExamplesLinear pairs, vertical angles and parallel lines

📋 Given
In Fig. 6.9, lines \(PQ\) and \(RS\) intersect at \(O\). If \(\angle POR:\angle ROQ=5:7\), find all four angles.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
A linear pair sums to \(180^\circ\), and vertically opposite angles are equal.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let \(\angle POR=5k\) and \(\angle ROQ=7k\).
  2. As they form a linear pair, \(5k+7k=180^\circ\).
  3. Thus \(12k=180^\circ\Rightarrow k=15^\circ\).
  4. \(\angle POR=75^\circ\) and \(\angle ROQ=105^\circ\).
  5. Vertically opposite angles give \(\angle SOQ=75^\circ\) and \(\angle POS=105^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
Angles are \(\boxed{75^\circ,105^\circ,75^\circ,105^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ખૂણાઓ: \(75^\circ,105^\circ,75^\circ,105^\circ\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.10, ray \(OS\) stands on line \(POQ\). Rays \(OR\) and \(OT\) bisect \(\angle POS\) and \(\angle SOQ\), respectively. If \(\angle POS=x\), find \(\angle ROT\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Linear-pair sum and angle-bisector definition.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle POS+\angle SOQ=180^\circ\), so \(\angle SOQ=180^\circ-x\).
  2. Because \(OR\) bisects \(\angle POS\), \(\angle ROS=\frac{x}{2}\).
  3. Because \(OT\) bisects \(\angle SOQ\), \(\angle SOT=\frac12(180^\circ-x)=90^\circ-\frac{x}{2}\).
  4. \(\angle ROT=\angle ROS+\angle SOT=\frac{x}{2}+90^\circ-\frac{x}{2}=90^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle ROT=90^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle ROT=90^\circ}\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.11, \(OP,OQ,OR,OS\) are four rays. Prove that \(\angle POQ+\angle QOR+\angle SOR+\angle POS=360^\circ\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Angles around a point make one complete revolution.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Produce ray \(OQ\) backwards to a point \(T\), so \(TOQ\) is a line.
  2. Ray \(OP\) stands on line \(TOQ\): \(\angle TOP+\angle POQ=180^\circ\).
  3. Ray \(OS\) also stands on line \(TOQ\): \(\angle TOS+\angle SOQ=180^\circ\).
  4. But \(\angle SOQ=\angle SOR+\angle ROQ\).
  5. Adding the two linear-pair equations gives the four angles around \(O\) equal to \(360^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle POQ+\angle QOR+\angle SOR+\angle POS=360^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
બિંદુની આસપાસના ખૂણાઓનો સરવાળો \(360^\circ\) છે.
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.19, \(PQ\parallel RS\), \(\angle QXM=135^\circ\) and \(\angle MYR=40^\circ\). Find \(\angle XMY\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Draw a line through \(M\) parallel to the two given parallel lines and use interior/alternate angles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Draw \(AB\) through \(M\) such that \(AB\parallel PQ\parallel RS\).
  2. Interior angles on the same side of transversal \(XM\): \(\angle QXM+\angle XMB=180^\circ\).
  3. Hence \(\angle XMB=180^\circ-135^\circ=45^\circ\).
  4. By alternate interior angles, \(\angle BMY=\angle MYR=40^\circ\).
  5. Therefore \(\angle XMY=45^\circ+40^\circ=85^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle XMY=85^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle XMY=85^\circ}\).
📋 Given
A transversal intersects lines \(PQ\) and \(RS\). The bisectors of a pair of corresponding angles are parallel. Prove that \(PQ\parallel RS\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
If bisected corresponding angles are equal, then the full corresponding angles are equal; use the converse of the corresponding-angles axiom.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let \(BE\) and \(CG\) bisect corresponding angles \(\angle ABQ\) and \(\angle BCS\).
  2. Then \(\angle ABE=\frac12\angle ABQ\) and \(\angle BCG=\frac12\angle BCS\).
  3. Because \(BE\parallel CG\), corresponding angles \(\angle ABE=\angle BCG\).
  4. Therefore \(\frac12\angle ABQ=\frac12\angle BCS\), so \(\angle ABQ=\angle BCS\).
  5. Equal corresponding angles imply \(PQ\parallel RS\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{PQ\parallel RS}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{PQ\parallel RS}\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.22, \(AB\parallel CD\), \(CD\parallel EF\), and \(EA\perp AB\). If \(\angle BEF=55^\circ\), find \(x,y,z\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Parallel-line angle relations and perpendicular-line right angle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. On transversal \(ED\), \(y+55^\circ=180^\circ\), so \(y=125^\circ\).
  2. Because \(AB\parallel CD\), corresponding angles give \(x=y=125^\circ\).
  3. Also \(AB\parallel EF\), and \(EA\perp AB\), hence \(EA\perp EF\).
  4. At \(E\), \(90^\circ=z+55^\circ\), so \(z=35^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{x=125^\circ,\;y=125^\circ,\;z=35^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(x=125^\circ, y=125^\circ, z=35^\circ\).

Exercise 6.1Intersecting lines and angle relations

📋 Given
In Fig. 6.13, lines \(AB\) and \(CD\) intersect at \(O\). If \(\angle AOC+\angle BOE=70^\circ\) and \(\angle BOD=40^\circ\), find \(\angle BOE\) and reflex \(\angle COE\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Vertically opposite angles, angle addition and reflex angle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle AOC=\angle BOD=40^\circ\) because they are vertically opposite.
  2. Given \(\angle AOC+\angle BOE=70^\circ\), so \(40^\circ+\angle BOE=70^\circ\).
  3. Hence \(\angle BOE=30^\circ\).
  4. \(\angle COB=180^\circ-40^\circ=140^\circ\).
  5. Minor \(\angle COE=140^\circ-30^\circ=110^\circ\).
  6. Reflex \(\angle COE=360^\circ-110^\circ=250^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle BOE=30^\circ,\;\text{reflex }\angle COE=250^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\angle BOE=30^\circ\), પ્રતિબિંબ ખૂણો \(COE=250^\circ\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.14, lines \(XY\) and \(MN\) intersect at \(O\). If \(\angle POY=90^\circ\) and \(a:b=2:3\), find \(c\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use the right angle and linear-pair/vertically opposite angle relations shown in the figure.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. From the figure, \(a+b=90^\circ\).
  2. Let \(a=2k\) and \(b=3k\).
  3. Then \(5k=90^\circ\Rightarrow k=18^\circ\).
  4. Thus \(a=36^\circ\) and \(b=54^\circ\).
  5. Angle \(c\) forms a linear pair with \(b\), so \(c=180^\circ-54^\circ=126^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{c=126^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{c=126^\circ}\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.15, \(\angle PQR=\angle PRQ\). Prove that \(\angle PQS=\angle PRT\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Angles forming linear pairs with equal angles are equal.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle PQS+\angle PQR=180^\circ\) because \(Q,R,S\) are collinear as shown.
  2. \(\angle PRT+\angle PRQ=180^\circ\).
  3. Given \(\angle PQR=\angle PRQ\).
  4. Subtracting equal angles from \(180^\circ\) gives \(\angle PQS=\angle PRT\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle PQS=\angle PRT}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle PQS=\angle PRT}\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.16, if \(x+y=w+z\), prove that \(AOB\) is a straight line.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Angles around a point total \(360^\circ\); if two adjacent groups each total \(180^\circ\), the common arms are opposite rays.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Around \(O\), \(x+y+w+z=360^\circ\).
  2. Given \(x+y=w+z\).
  3. Therefore \(2(x+y)=360^\circ\Rightarrow x+y=180^\circ\).
  4. The angle \(\angle AOB=x+y=180^\circ\), so rays \(OA\) and \(OB\) are opposite.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AOB\text{ is a straight line}}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
AOB સીધી રેખા છે.
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.17, \(POQ\) is a line, \(OR\perp PQ\), and \(OS\) lies between \(OP\) and \(OR\). Prove that \(\angle ROS=\frac12(\angle QOS-\angle POS)\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use right angles and angle addition.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let \(\angle POS=x\). Since \(OR\perp PQ\), \(\angle POR=90^\circ\).
  2. Thus \(\angle ROS=90^\circ-x\).
  3. Because \(POQ\) is a line, \(\angle QOS=180^\circ-x\).
  4. \(\angle QOS-\angle POS=(180^\circ-x)-x=180^\circ-2x\).
  5. Half of this is \(90^\circ-x=\angle ROS\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle ROS=\frac12(\angle QOS-\angle POS)}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle ROS=\frac12(\angle QOS-\angle POS)}\).
📋 Given
It is given that \(\angle XYZ=64^\circ\) and \(XY\) is produced to \(P\). Ray \(YQ\) bisects \(\angle ZYP\). Find \(\angle XYQ\) and reflex \(\angle QYP\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Linear pair and angle bisector.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle XYZ+\angle ZYP=180^\circ\).
  2. \(\angle ZYP=180^\circ-64^\circ=116^\circ\).
  3. Because \(YQ\) bisects \(\angle ZYP\), \(\angle QYP=\angle ZYQ=58^\circ\).
  4. \(\angle XYQ=\angle XYZ+\angle ZYQ=64^\circ+58^\circ=122^\circ\).
  5. Reflex \(\angle QYP=360^\circ-58^\circ=302^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle XYQ=122^\circ,\;\text{reflex }\angle QYP=302^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\angle XYQ=122^\circ\), પ્રતિબિંબ \(\angle QYP=302^\circ\).

Exercise 6.2Parallel lines and transversals

📋 Given
In Fig. 6.23, \(AB\parallel CD\), \(CD\parallel EF\), and \(y:z=3:7\). Find \(x\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Lines parallel to the same line are parallel; use linear pairs and corresponding angles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Because \(AB\parallel CD\parallel EF\), angles \(y\) and \(z\) are interior on the same side, so \(y+z=180^\circ\).
  2. Let \(y=3k,z=7k\). Then \(10k=180^\circ\Rightarrow k=18^\circ\).
  3. Thus \(y=54^\circ\) and \(z=126^\circ\).
  4. Angle \(x\) is corresponding to \(z\), hence \(x=126^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{x=126^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{x=126^\circ}\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.24, \(AB\parallel CD\), \(EF\perp CD\), and \(\angle GED=126^\circ\). Find \(\angle AGE\), \(\angle GEF\) and \(\angle FGE\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Parallel lines, right angle and triangle angle sum.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Because \(AB\parallel CD\), the angle made by transversal \(GE\) gives \(\angle AGE=126^\circ\).
  2. Since \(EF\perp CD\), \(\angle DEF=90^\circ\).
  3. At \(E\), \(\angle GED=126^\circ\), so the acute angle \(\angle GEF=126^\circ-90^\circ=36^\circ\).
  4. In triangle \(GEF\), \(\angle FGE=180^\circ-90^\circ-36^\circ=54^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle AGE=126^\circ,\;\angle GEF=36^\circ,\;\angle FGE=54^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(126^\circ,36^\circ,54^\circ\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.25, \(PQ\parallel ST\), \(\angle PQR=110^\circ\) and \(\angle RST=130^\circ\). Find \(\angle QRS\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Draw a line through \(R\) parallel to \(PQ\) and \(ST\), then split the required angle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Draw a line through \(R\) parallel to \(PQ\parallel ST\).
  2. The angle between \(RQ\) and this parallel is \(180^\circ-110^\circ=70^\circ\).
  3. The angle between this parallel and \(RS\) is \(180^\circ-130^\circ=50^\circ\).
  4. Therefore \(\angle QRS=70^\circ+50^\circ=120^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle QRS=120^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle QRS=120^\circ}\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.26, \(AB\parallel CD\), \(\angle APQ=50^\circ\) and \(\angle PRD=127^\circ\). Find \(x\) and \(y\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use alternate/corresponding angles and linear pairs.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Because \(AB\parallel CD\), the angle corresponding/alternate to \(\angle APQ\) equals \(50^\circ\); hence \(x=50^\circ\).
  2. At \(R\), \(\angle PRD=127^\circ\) and the adjacent angle is \(180^\circ-127^\circ=53^\circ\).
  3. Using the triangle angle sum with the \(50^\circ\) angle gives \(y=180^\circ-50^\circ-53^\circ=77^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{x=50^\circ,\;y=77^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(x=50^\circ, y=77^\circ\).
📋 Given
In Fig. 6.27, a ray of light is reflected successively from two parallel mirrors. Prove that the incident ray \(AB\) is parallel to the reflected ray \(CD\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Law of reflection: angle of incidence equals angle of reflection; normals to parallel mirrors are parallel.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Draw normals at the two points of reflection.
  2. Because the mirrors are parallel, their normals are parallel.
  3. At the first mirror, angle of incidence equals angle of reflection.
  4. At the second mirror, the corresponding incidence and reflection angles are also equal.
  5. Therefore the initial ray and final ray make equal corresponding angles with two parallel normals.
  6. Hence \(AB\parallel CD\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB\parallel CD}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB\parallel CD}\).
