Solved ExamplesChords, arcs and cyclic quadrilaterals

📋 Given
Chords \(AB\) and \(CD\) intersect at \(E\). Diameter \(PQ\) passes through \(E\), and \(\angle AEQ=\angle DEQ\). Perpendiculars \(OL\perp AB\) and \(OM\perp CD\) are drawn.
🎯 To Find
Prove that \(AB=CD\).
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Equal angles give congruent right triangles; equal distances from the centre imply equal chords.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. In right triangles \(OLE\) and \(OME\), \(\angle LEO=\angle MEO\) from the given equal angles.
  2. Also \(\angle LOE=90^\circ-\angle LEO=90^\circ-\angle MEO=\angle MOE\).
  3. \(EO=EO\) is common.
  4. Therefore \(\triangle OLE\cong\triangle OME\) by ASA.
  5. Hence \(OL=OM\) by CPCT.
  6. Chords equidistant from the centre are equal.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=CD}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=CD}\).
📋 Given
\(AB\) is a diameter and \(CD\) equals the radius. Lines \(AC\) and \(BD\) meet at \(E\).
🎯 To Find
Prove \(\angle AEB=60^\circ\).
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use an equilateral triangle, the angle-at-centre theorem, and the angle in a semicircle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Join \(OC,OD\) and \(BC\).
  2. \(OC=OD=CD\), so \(\triangle OCD\) is equilateral and \(\angle COD=60^\circ\).
  3. \(\angle CBD=\frac12\angle COD=30^\circ\).
  4. Because \(AB\) is a diameter, \(\angle ACB=90^\circ\).
  5. Since \(A,C,E\) are collinear, \(\angle BCE=180^\circ-90^\circ=90^\circ\).
  6. In \(\triangle CEB\), \(\angle CEB=180^\circ-90^\circ-30^\circ=60^\circ\).
  7. As \(EA\) and \(EC\) are the same ray, \(\angle AEB=\angle CEB\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle AEB=60^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle AEB=60^\circ}\).
📋 Given
\(ABCD\) is cyclic, \(\angle DBC=55^\circ\), and \(\angle BAC=45^\circ\).
🎯 To Find
Find \(\angle BCD\).
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Angles in the same segment are equal; opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle CAD=\angle CBD=55^\circ\), because both subtend chord \(CD\).
  2. \(\angle DAB=\angle DAC+\angle CAB=55^\circ+45^\circ=100^\circ\).
  3. Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral have sum \(180^\circ\).
  4. \(\angle BCD=180^\circ-\angle DAB=180^\circ-100^\circ=80^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle BCD=80^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle BCD=80^\circ}\).
📋 Given
Two circles intersect at \(A,B\); \(AD\) and \(AC\) are their diameters.
🎯 To Find
Prove that \(D,B,C\) are collinear.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
An angle in a semicircle is a right angle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Join \(AB\).
  2. Because \(AD\) is a diameter, \(\angle ABD=90^\circ\).
  3. Because \(AC\) is a diameter, \(\angle ABC=90^\circ\).
  4. \(\angle ABD+\angle ABC=180^\circ\).
  5. Therefore rays \(BD\) and \(BC\) form a straight line.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{D,B,C\text{ are collinear}}\); hence \(B\) lies on \(DC\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(D,B,C\) એકરેખીય છે; તેથી \(B\), \(DC\) પર આવેલ છે.
📋 Given
In quadrilateral \(ABCD\), the internal angle bisectors meet to form quadrilateral \(EFGH\).
🎯 To Find
Prove that \(EFGH\) is cyclic.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
A quadrilateral is cyclic if a pair of opposite angles is supplementary.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle FEH=180^\circ-\frac12(\angle A+\angle B)\).
  2. \(\angle FGH=180^\circ-\frac12(\angle C+\angle D)\).
  3. Add the two equations.
  4. \(\angle FEH+\angle FGH=360^\circ-\frac12(\angle A+\angle B+\angle C+\angle D)\).
  5. The sum of the angles of quadrilateral \(ABCD\) is \(360^\circ\).
  6. Therefore \(\angle FEH+\angle FGH=360^\circ-180^\circ=180^\circ\).
  7. A pair of opposite angles of \(EFGH\) is supplementary.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{EFGH\text{ is cyclic}}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(EFGH\) ચક્રીય ચતુષ્કોણ છે.

Exercise 9.1Equal chords and central angles

📋 Given
Recall that two circles are congruent if they have equal radii. Prove that equal chords of congruent circles subtend equal angles at their centres.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SSS congruence across the two congruent circles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let equal chords be \(AB\) and \(CD\), with centres \(O\) and \(P\).
  2. Congruent circles have equal radii: \(OA=OB=PC=PD\).
  3. Also \(AB=CD\) is given.
  4. Thus \(\triangle AOB\cong\triangle CPD\) by SSS.
  5. Hence \(\angle AOB=\angle CPD\).
✅ Final Answer
Equal chords of congruent circles subtend equal central angles.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
સર્વાંગસમ વર્તુળોની સમાન જીવાઓ કેન્દ્ર પર સમાન ખૂણા બનાવે છે.
📋 Given
Prove that if chords of congruent circles subtend equal angles at their centres, then the chords are equal.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SSS/SAS congruence using equal radii and included central angle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let \(\angle AOB=\angle CPD\).
  2. Radii satisfy \(OA=PC\) and \(OB=PD\).
  3. The included angles are equal.
  4. Therefore \(\triangle AOB\cong\triangle CPD\) by SAS.
  5. Hence \(AB=CD\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=CD}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=CD}\).

Exercise 9.2Distance of chords from the centre

📋 Given
Two circles of radii 5 cm and 3 cm intersect at two points, and the distance between their centres is 4 cm. Find the length of the common chord.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
The line of centres perpendicularly bisects the common chord; apply Pythagoras.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let centres be \(O_1,O_2\), and common chord meet \(O_1O_2\) at \(M\).
  2. Let \(O_1M=x\), so \(O_2M=4-x\), and let half-chord be \(h\).
  3. \(h^2+x^2=5^2\) and \(h^2+(4-x)^2=3^2\).
  4. Subtracting gives \(x^2-(4-x)^2=16\Rightarrow 8x-16=16\Rightarrow x=4\).
  5. Thus \(h^2=25-16=9\Rightarrow h=3\).
  6. Common chord length \(=2h=6\) cm.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{6\text{ cm}}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{6\text{ સેમી}}\).
📋 Given
If two equal chords of a circle intersect within the circle, prove that the segments of one chord are equal to the corresponding segments of the other chord.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use equal-chord lengths and the intersecting-chords relation.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let equal chords \(AB\) and \(CD\) intersect at \(P\). Write \(AP=x,PB=y,CP=u,PD=v\).
  2. Equal chords give \(x+y=u+v\).
  3. Intersecting chords give \(xy=uv\).
  4. Two pairs with the same sum and product are the roots of the same quadratic.
  5. Therefore the unordered pairs are equal: \(\{x,y\}=\{u,v\}\), giving corresponding segments equal.
✅ Final Answer
The corresponding segments of the two equal chords are equal.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
સમાન જીવાઓના અનુરૂપ ખંડો સમાન છે.
📋 Given
If two equal chords of a circle intersect within the circle, prove that the line joining the point of intersection to the centre makes equal angles with the chords.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Equal chords are equidistant from the centre; use congruent right triangles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let equal chords meet at \(P\) and centre be \(O\). Draw perpendiculars \(OM,ON\) to the chords.
  2. Equal chords imply \(OM=ON\).
  3. Right triangles \(OMP\) and \(ONP\) have common hypotenuse \(OP\) and equal legs \(OM=ON\).
  4. By RHS, they are congruent.
  5. Hence the angles made by \(OP\) with the two chords are equal.
✅ Final Answer
The line from the intersection to the centre bisects the angle between the chords.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
છેદબિંદુ અને કેન્દ્રને જોડતી રેખા જીવાઓ વચ્ચેનો ખૂણો દ્વિભાજિત કરે છે.
📋 Given
A line intersects two concentric circles with centre \(O\) at \(A,B,C,D\). Prove that \(AB=CD\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
The perpendicular from the common centre bisects both chords.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Draw \(OM\perp AD\).
  2. For the larger circle, \(AM=MD\).
  3. For the smaller circle, \(BM=MC\).
  4. \(AB=AM-BM\) and \(CD=DM-CM\).
  5. Using \(AM=DM\) and \(BM=CM\), obtain \(AB=CD\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=CD}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=CD}\).
📋 Given
Reshma, Salma and Mandip stand on a circle of radius 5 m. The distances Reshma–Salma and Salma–Mandip are each 6 m. Find the distance Reshma–Mandip.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use circumradius formula or perpendicular-bisector geometry in the isosceles triangle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let the required chord \(RM=2x\). The altitude from \(S\) bisects it, so half-base is \(x\).
  2. If the altitude is \(h\), then \(x^2+h^2=6^2\).
  3. For a triangle with sides \(6,6,2x\), area \(=xh\) and circumradius \(R=\frac{6\cdot6\cdot2x}{4xh}=\frac{18}{h}\).
  4. Given \(R=5\), so \(h=\frac{18}{5}=3.6\).
  5. \(x^2=36-12.96=23.04\Rightarrow x=4.8\).
  6. Thus \(RM=2x=9.6\) m.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{9.6\text{ m}}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{9.6\text{ મીટર}}\).
📋 Given
A circular park has radius 20 m. Three boys sit at equal distances on its boundary and use string telephones. Find the length of each string.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
The three points form an equilateral triangle inscribed in a circle; its circumradius satisfies \(R=a/\sqrt3\).
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Equal spacing divides the circle into three equal arcs, so the triangle is equilateral.
  2. For an equilateral triangle of side \(a\), circumradius \(R=\frac{a}{\sqrt3}\).
  3. With \(R=20\), \(a=20\sqrt3\) m.
✅ Final Answer
Each string is \(\boxed{20\sqrt3\text{ m}}\) (about \(34.64\) m).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
દરેક દોરી \(20\sqrt3\) મીટર છે.

Exercise 9.3Angles subtended by arcs

📋 Given
In Fig. 9.23, \(\angle BOC=30^\circ\) and \(\angle AOB=60^\circ\). If \(D\) is a point on the circle other than the arc \(ABC\), find \(\angle ADC\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
An angle at the circumference is half the central angle standing on the same arc.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle AOC=\angle AOB+\angle BOC=60^\circ+30^\circ=90^\circ\).
  2. \(\angle ADC\) subtends the same arc \(AC\).
  3. Therefore \(\angle ADC=\frac12\angle AOC=45^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{45^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{45^\circ}\).
📋 Given
A chord of a circle is equal to its radius. Find the angle subtended by this chord at a point on the minor arc and at a point on the major arc.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
The chord and two radii form an equilateral triangle; use the relation between central and inscribed angles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. If chord \(AB=OA=OB\), triangle \(AOB\) is equilateral, so minor central angle \(AOB=60^\circ\).
  2. At a point on the major arc, the chord subtends half the minor arc: \(30^\circ\).
  3. At a point on the minor arc, it intercepts the major arc \(360^\circ-60^\circ=300^\circ\), giving \(150^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
Minor-arc point: \(\boxed{150^\circ}\); major-arc point: \(\boxed{30^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
નાના ચાપ પર 150°, મોટા ચાપ પર 30°.
📋 Given
In Fig. 9.24, \(\angle PQR=100^\circ\), where \(P,Q,R\) lie on a circle with centre \(O\). Find \(\angle OPR\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
The inscribed angle determines the reflex central angle; then use the isosceles triangle of radii.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle PQR=100^\circ\) intercepts the major arc \(PR\) of measure \(200^\circ\).
  2. The minor central angle \(\angle POR=360^\circ-200^\circ=160^\circ\).
  3. In isosceles \(\triangle POR\), \(OP=OR\).
  4. Each base angle is \(\frac{180^\circ-160^\circ}{2}=10^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle OPR=10^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer

📋 Given
\(ABCD\) is a cyclic quadrilateral whose diagonals intersect at \(E\). If \(\angle DBC=70^\circ\) and \(\angle BAC=30^\circ\), find \(\angle BCD\). Further, if \(AB=BC\), find \(\angle ECD\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Same-segment angles, triangle angle sum and isosceles-triangle angles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(\angle DAC=\angle DBC=70^\circ\) because both stand on chord \(DC\).
  2. Thus \(\angle DAB=\angle DAC+\angle CAB=70^\circ+30^\circ=100^\circ\).
  3. Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary: \(\angle BCD=180^\circ-100^\circ=80^\circ\).
  4. If \(AB=BC\), triangle \(ABC\) is isosceles and \(\angle BAC=\angle ACB=30^\circ\).
  5. At \(C\), \(\angle BCD=\angle BCA+\angle ACD\), so \(\angle ECD=\angle ACD=80^\circ-30^\circ=50^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle BCD=80^\circ,\;\angle ECD=50^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(80^\circ\) અને \(50^\circ\).
📋 Given
If the diagonals of a cyclic quadrilateral are diameters of its circumcircle, prove that the quadrilateral is a rectangle.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Angle in a semicircle is a right angle.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Each angle of the quadrilateral stands on one of the diameter diagonals.
  2. An angle subtended by a diameter at the circumference is \(90^\circ\).
  3. Thus all four angles are right angles.
  4. A quadrilateral with four right angles is a rectangle.
✅ Final Answer
The cyclic quadrilateral is a rectangle.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ચક્રીય ચતુર્ભુજ લંબચોરસ છે.
📋 Given
If the non-parallel sides of a trapezium are equal, prove that it is cyclic.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Base angles of an isosceles trapezium are equal; combine with supplementary interior angles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let \(AB\parallel CD\) and \(AD=BC\). Then the trapezium is isosceles, so \(\angle A=\angle B\).
  2. Because \(AB\parallel CD\), \(\angle B+\angle C=180^\circ\).
  3. Replacing \(\angle B\) by \(\angle A\) gives \(\angle A+\angle C=180^\circ\).
  4. A quadrilateral with a pair of opposite supplementary angles is cyclic.
✅ Final Answer
The trapezium is cyclic.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
સમદ્વિબાજુ સમલંબ ચતુર્ભુજ ચક્રીય છે.

Two circles intersect at \(B\) and \(C\). Through \(B\), two line segments \(ABD\) and \(PBQ\) are drawn to meet the circles at \(A,D\) and \(P,Q\). Prove that \(\angle ACP=\angle QCD\).

\(\boxed{\angle ACP=\angle QCD}\).

📋 Given
Prove that a cyclic parallelogram is a rectangle.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal; opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Let one pair of opposite angles be \(A\) and \(C\).
  2. Parallelogram property: \(A=C\).
  3. Cyclic property: \(A+C=180^\circ\).
  4. Thus \(2A=180^\circ\Rightarrow A=C=90^\circ\).
  5. All angles are right angles, so the parallelogram is a rectangle.
✅ Final Answer
A cyclic parallelogram is a rectangle.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ચક્રીય સમાંતરબાજુ ચતુર્ભુજ લંબચોરસ છે.
