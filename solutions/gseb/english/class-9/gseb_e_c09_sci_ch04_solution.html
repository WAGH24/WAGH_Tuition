Maximum electrons in shell number \(n\) is \(2n^2\). Thus K holds 2, L 8, M 18 theoretically. The outermost shell can have at most 8 electrons. Electrons fill inner shells first; a new shell starts only after the inner one is filled. For the first 18 elements, the M shell is filled up to 8.
