📋 Given

Mass of solute = 36 g; mass of water = 100 g; mass of solution = 136 g.

📘 Formula Used

\(\text{Mass percentage}=\dfrac{\text{mass of solute}}{\text{mass of solution}}\times100\)

✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Mass of solution \(=36+100=136\,\text{g}\).
  2. \(\text{Concentration}=\dfrac{36}{136}\times100\).
  3. \(\text{Concentration}=26.47\%\) by mass.
✅ Final Answer

The concentration of the saturated sodium chloride solution is approximately \(26.5\%\) by mass.

✅ Answer

(a)

At 313 K, 62 g KNO₃ dissolves in 100 g water. For 50 g water: \(62\times50/100=31\,g\).

(b)

On cooling, the solubility of KCl decreases, so excess KCl crystallises out.

(c)

At 293 K: KNO₃ = 32 g, NaCl = 36 g, KCl = 35 g, NH₄Cl = 37 g per 100 g water. NH₄Cl has the highest solubility.

(d)

For these salts, solubility generally increases with temperature; the increase is very small for NaCl and much larger for KNO₃.

✅ Final Answer

(a) At 313 K, 62 g KNO₃ dissolves in 100 g water. For 50 g water: \(62\times50/100=31\,g\). (b) On cooling, the solubility of KCl decreases, so excess KCl crystallises out. (c) At 293 K: KNO₃ = 32 g, NaCl = 36 g, KCl = 35 g, NH₄Cl = 37 g per 100 g water. NH₄Cl has the highest solubility. (d) For these salts, solubility generally increases with temperature; the increase is very small for NaCl and much larger for KNO₃.

🌐 Gujarati Translation

(a) 31 g KNO₃. (b) KClના સ્ફટિકો બહાર પડશે. (c) 293 K પર NH₄Cl સૌથી વધુ દ્રાવ્ય (37 g/100 g પાણી) છે. (d) સામાન્ય રીતે તાપમાન વધે તેમ દ્રાવ્યતા વધે છે.

✅ Answer

(a) Saturated solution

A solution that cannot dissolve more solute at a given temperature; e.g., saturated salt solution.

(b) Pure substance

Matter with fixed composition and one kind of particles; e.g., distilled water or oxygen.

(c) Colloid

A heterogeneous mixture with intermediate particle size that scatters light but does not settle; e.g., milk.

(d) Suspension

A heterogeneous mixture with large particles that settle and can be filtered; e.g., chalk powder in water.

✅ Final Answer

(a) Saturated solution A solution that cannot dissolve more solute at a given temperature; e.g., saturated salt solution. (b) Pure substance Matter with fixed composition and one kind of particles; e.g., distilled water or oxygen. (c) Colloid A heterogeneous mixture with intermediate particle size that scatters light but does not settle; e.g., milk. (d) Suspension A heterogeneous mixture with large particles that settle and can be filtered; e.g., chalk powder in water.

Measure its boiling point at normal atmospheric pressure. Pure water boils sharply at 100°C (373 K) and freezes at 0°C (273 K). If the liquid has a constant boiling/freezing point matching these values and leaves no residue on evaporation, it is pure water.
