📋 Given
In Fig. 7.8, \(OA=OB\) and \(OD=OC\). Show that (i) \(\triangle AOD\cong\triangle BOC\), and (ii) \(AD\parallel BC\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SAS congruence followed by equality of alternate interior angles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. In \(\triangle AOD\) and \(\triangle BOC\), \(OA=OB\) and \(OD=OC\) are given.
  2. \(\angle AOD=\angle BOC\) because they are vertically opposite.
  3. Therefore \(\triangle AOD\cong\triangle BOC\) by SAS.
  4. Corresponding angles \(\angle OAD=\angle OBC\).
  5. Equal alternate interior angles imply \(AD\parallel BC\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\triangle AOD\cong\triangle BOC\text{ and }AD\parallel BC}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\triangle AOD\cong\triangle BOC\) અને \(AD\parallel BC\).
📋 Given

📋 Given

SAS congruence after obtaining equal base subsegments.

\(\boxed{AD=AE}\).

📋 Given
\(P\) and \(Q\) lie on opposite sides of line segment \(AB\), and each is equidistant from \(A\) and \(B\). Show that \(PQ\) is the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SSS congruence and linear-pair argument.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Given \(PA=PB\) and \(QA=QB\).
  2. In \(\triangle PAQ\) and \(\triangle PBQ\): \(PA=PB\), \(QA=QB\), and \(PQ\) is common.
  3. Therefore the triangles are congruent by SSS, so \(\angle APQ=\angle QPB\).
  4. Let \(PQ\) meet \(AB\) at \(C\). Applying congruence to \(\triangle PCA\) and \(\triangle PCB\) gives \(AC=BC\) and equal adjacent angles at \(C\).

✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{PQ\perp AB\text{ and }AC=CB}\); hence \(PQ\) is the perpendicular bisector.

📋 Given

Exercise 7.1Congruence criteria

📋 Given
In quadrilateral \(ACBD\), \(AC=AD\) and \(AB\) bisects \(\angle A\). Show that (i) \(\triangle ABC\cong\triangle ABD\), and (ii) \(BC=BD\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SAS congruence and CPCT.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(AC=AD\) is given.
  2. \(AB=AB\) is common.
  3. \(\angle CAB=\angle BAD\) because \(AB\) bisects \(\angle CAD\).
  4. Thus \(\triangle ABC\cong\triangle ABD\) by SAS.
  5. Hence \(BC=BD\) by CPCT.
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\triangle ABC\cong\triangle ABD\text{ and }BC=BD}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
બંને ત્રિકોણ સર્વાંગસમ અને \(BC=BD\).
📋 Given
In quadrilateral \(ABCD\), \(AD=BC\) and \(\angle DAB=\angle CBA\). Prove that (i) \(\triangle ABD\cong\triangle BAC\), (ii) \(BD=AC\), and (iii) \(\angle ABD=\angle BAC\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SAS congruence and CPCT.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(AD=BC\), \(AB=BA\), and included angles \(\angle DAB=\angle CBA\).
  2. Therefore \(\triangle DAB\cong\triangle CBA\) by SAS.
  3. Corresponding sides give \(BD=AC\).
  4. Corresponding angles give \(\angle ABD=\angle BAC\).
✅ Final Answer
All three required results follow by SAS and CPCT.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ત્રણેય પરિણામો SAS અને CPCTથી સાબિત થાય છે.

Exercise 7.2Properties of isosceles triangles

📋 Given
In isosceles \(\triangle ABC\), \(AB=AC\). The bisectors of \(\angle B\) and \(\angle C\) meet at \(O\). Join \(AO\). Show that (i) \(OB=OC\), and (ii) \(AO\) bisects \(\angle A\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Base-angle theorem and congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Since \(AB=AC\), \(\angle ABC=\angle BCA\).
  2. Their halves are equal: \(\angle OBC=\angle BCO\).
  3. In \(\triangle BOC\), equal base angles imply \(OB=OC\).
  4. Now in \(\triangle AOB\) and \(\triangle AOC\): \(AB=AC\), \(OB=OC\), and \(AO\) common.
  5. By SSS the triangles are congruent, so \(\angle BAO=\angle OAC\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{OB=OC\text{ and }AO\text{ bisects }\angle A}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
OB=OC અને AO, ∠Aને દ્વિભાજિત કરે છે.
📋 Given
In \(\triangle ABC\), \(AD\) is the perpendicular bisector of \(BC\). Show that \(AB=AC\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
RHS congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(BD=DC\) because \(AD\) bisects \(BC\).
  2. \(\angle ADB=\angle ADC=90^\circ\).
  3. \(AD\) is common.
  4. Right triangles \(ABD\) and \(ACD\) are congruent by SAS/RHS.
  5. Hence \(AB=AC\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=AC}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=AC}\).
📋 Given
\(ABC\) is an isosceles triangle with \(AB=AC\). Altitudes \(BE\) and \(CF\) are drawn to equal sides \(AC\) and \(AB\), respectively. Show that \(BE=CF\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Congruence of right triangles.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Consider right triangles \(\triangle ABE\) and \(\triangle ACF\).
  2. Their hypotenuses satisfy \(AB=AC\).
  3. \(\angle BAE=\angle CAF=\angle A\).
  4. Thus they are congruent by AAS/RHS.
  5. Therefore \(BE=CF\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{BE=CF}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{BE=CF}\).
📋 Given
\(BE\) and \(CF\) are equal altitudes of \(\triangle ABC\). Using RHS congruence, prove that \(\triangle ABC\) is isosceles.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
RHS congruence of triangles containing the equal altitudes.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Consider right triangles \(\triangle BEC\) and \(\triangle CFB\).
  2. \(BE=CF\) is given and \(BC\) is the common hypotenuse.
  3. They are congruent by RHS, giving \(\angle BCE=\angle CBF\), i.e. \(\angle C=\angle B\).
  4. Sides opposite equal angles are equal, so \(AB=AC\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{AB=AC}\); \(\triangle ABC\) is isosceles.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
AB=AC, તેથી ત્રિકોણ સમદ્વિબાજુ છે.
📋 Given
\(ABC\) and \(DBC\) are two isosceles triangles on the same base \(BC\). Show that \(\angle ABD=\angle ACD\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SSS congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. In \(\triangle ABD\) and \(\triangle ACD\), \(AB=AC\) and \(DB=DC\) because the two triangles are isosceles.
  2. \(AD=AD\) is common.
  3. Thus the triangles are congruent by SSS.
  4. Corresponding angles give \(\angle ABD=\angle ACD\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle ABD=\angle ACD}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle ABD=\angle ACD}\).
📋 Given
\(ABC\) is isosceles with \(AB=AC\). Side \(BA\) is produced to \(D\) such that \(AD=AB\). Show that \(\angle BCD=90^\circ\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Equal-side angle relations and exterior angle theorem.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Because \(AB=AC\), let \(\angle ABC=\angle BCA=x\). Then \(\angle BAC=180^\circ-2x\).
  2. Since \(BA\) is produced, \(\angle CAD=180^\circ-\angle CAB=2x\).
  3. Given \(AD=AB=AC\), triangle \(ACD\) is isosceles with \(AC=AD\).
  4. Thus \(\angle ACD=\angle CDA=\frac{180^\circ-2x}{2}=90^\circ-x\).
  5. \(\angle BCD=\angle BCA+\angle ACD=x+(90^\circ-x)=90^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle BCD=90^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle BCD=90^\circ}\).
📋 Given
\(ABC\) is a right isosceles triangle with \(\angle A=90^\circ\) and \(AB=AC\). Find \(\angle B\) and \(\angle C\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Angles opposite equal sides are equal; triangle angle sum.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(AB=AC\Rightarrow \angle B=\angle C\).
  2. \(\angle B+\angle C=180^\circ-90^\circ=90^\circ\).
  3. Each equals \(45^\circ\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{\angle B=\angle C=45^\circ}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
\(\angle B=\angle C=45^\circ\).

Exercise 7.3Applications of congruence

📋 Given
Two isosceles triangles \(ABC\) and \(DBC\) stand on the same base \(BC\) and on the same side. \(AD\) intersects \(BC\) at \(P\). Prove: (i) \(\triangle ABD\cong\triangle ACD\); (ii) \(\triangle ABP\cong\triangle ACP\); (iii) \(AP\) bisects \(\angle A\) and \(\angle D\); (iv) \(AP\) is the perpendicular bisector of \(BC\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Repeated SSS/SAS congruence and CPCT.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. (i) \(AB=AC\), \(DB=DC\), and \(AD\) common, so \(\triangle ABD\cong\triangle ACD\) by SSS.
  2. Hence \(\angle BAD=\angle DAC\) and \(\angle BDA=\angle ADC\).
  3. (ii) In \(\triangle ABP\) and \(\triangle ACP\), \(AB=AC\), \(AP\) common, and \(\angle BAP=\angle PAC\); so congruent by SAS.
  4. Thus \(BP=PC\) and \(\angle APB=\angle APC\).
  5. These equal adjacent angles form a linear pair, so both are \(90^\circ\).
  6. Therefore \(AP\) bisects both vertex angles and is perpendicular to and bisects \(BC\).
✅ Final Answer
All four statements are proved.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
ચારેય નિવેદનો સાબિત થાય છે.

📋 Given
Two sides \(AB,BC\) and median \(AM\) of \(\triangle ABC\) are respectively equal to sides \(PQ,QR\) and median \(PN\) of \(\triangle PQR\). Show that (i) \(\triangle ABM\cong\triangle PQN\), and (ii) \(\triangle ABC\cong\triangle PQR\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
SSS congruence.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Given \(AB=PQ\), \(BC=QR\), and \(AM=PN\).
  2. Because \(M,N\) are midpoints, \(BM=\frac12BC=\frac12QR=QN\).
  3. Thus \(\triangle ABM\cong\triangle PQN\) by SSS.
  4. CPCT gives \(\angle ABM=\angle PQN\), which are the full angles \(B\) and \(Q\).
  5. With \(AB=PQ\), \(BC=QR\), and included angles equal, \(\triangle ABC\cong\triangle PQR\) by SAS.
✅ Final Answer
Both required congruences are established.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
બંને આવશ્યક સર્વાંગસમતા સાબિત થાય છે.
