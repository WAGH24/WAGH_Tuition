Solved ExamplesReading and plotting coordinates

📋 Given
From the coordinate graph in Fig. 3.11, write the coordinates of the marked points \(B, M, L\) and \(S\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
For a point \(P(x,y)\), the first coordinate is the signed horizontal distance from the y-axis and the second coordinate is the signed vertical distance from the x-axis.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. For \(B\), move 4 units right and 3 units up, so \(B=(4,3)\).
  2. For \(M\), move 3 units left and 4 units up, so \(M=(-3,4)\).
  3. For \(L\), move 5 units left and 4 units down, so \(L=(-5,-4)\).
  4. For \(S\), move 2 units right and 4 units down, so \(S=(2,-4)\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{B(4,3),\;M(-3,4),\;L(-5,-4),\;S(2,-4)}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
અંતિમ જવાબ: \(B(4,3),\;M(-3,4),\;L(-5,-4),\;S(2,-4)\).
📋 Given
Write the coordinates of the points lying on the coordinate axes in Fig. 3.12 and write the coordinates of the origin.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
A point on the x-axis has ordinate \(0\); a point on the y-axis has abscissa \(0\); the origin is \((0,0)\).
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Point \(A\) is 4 units to the right on the x-axis: \(A=(4,0)\).
  2. Point \(B\) is 3 units above on the y-axis: \(B=(0,3)\).
  3. Point \(C\) is 5 units to the left on the x-axis: \(C=(-5,0)\).
  4. Point \(D\) is 4 units below on the y-axis: \(D=(0,-4)\).
  5. The axes meet at \(O=(0,0)\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{A(4,0),\;B(0,3),\;C(-5,0),\;D(0,-4),\;O(0,0)}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer

Exercise 3.1Introduction and street plan

📋 Given
How will you describe the position of a table lamp on your study table to another person?
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
The position of an object in a plane is fixed by two independent distances measured from two mutually perpendicular reference edges.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Choose two adjacent perpendicular edges of the table as reference lines.
  2. Measure the lamp’s perpendicular distance from the first edge; call it \(x\).
  3. Measure its perpendicular distance from the second edge; call it \(y\).
  4. State the position as the ordered pair \((x,y)\), using the same unit for both measurements.
✅ Final Answer
Describe the lamp by an ordered pair of perpendicular distances from two fixed adjacent edges of the table.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
દીવાના સ્થાનને ટેબલની બે પરસ્પર લંબ નિશ્ચિત ધારોથીના ક્રમબદ્ધ બે અંતર \((x,y)\) વડે દર્શાવવું.
📋 Given
Street Plan: A city has two main roads crossing at the centre, one North–South and the other East–West. All other streets are parallel to these roads, 200 m apart, with five streets in each direction. A cross-street is named by an ordered pair. Find:
(i) how many cross-streets can be referred to as \((4,3)\);
(ii) how many cross-streets can be referred to as \((3,4)\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
An ordered pair identifies exactly one intersection: first the North–South street number and then the East–West street number.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. For \((4,3)\), select the fourth North–South street and the third East–West street.
  2. These two fixed streets meet at only one point, so there is exactly one such cross-street.
  3. For \((3,4)\), select the third North–South street and the fourth East–West street.
  4. These also meet at exactly one point.
  5. The order matters: \((4,3)\neq(3,4)\), so the two intersections are different.
✅ Final Answer
(i) \(\boxed{1}\) cross-street; (ii) \(\boxed{1}\) cross-street, and the two are distinct.
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
(i) \(1\) ક્રોસ-સ્ટ્રીટ; (ii) \(1\) ક્રોસ-સ્ટ્રીટ; બંને અલગ છે.

Exercise 3.2Cartesian system and reading points

📋 Given
Answer the following:
(i) What is the name of the horizontal line and the vertical line drawn to determine the position of any point in the Cartesian plane?
(ii) What is the name of each part of the plane formed by these two lines?
(iii) Write the name of the point where these two lines intersect.
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Use the standard terminology of the Cartesian coordinate system.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. (i) The horizontal line is the x-axis and the vertical line is the y-axis.
  2. (ii) The four parts are called quadrants, numbered I, II, III and IV anticlockwise.
  3. (iii) Their intersection is called the origin, written \(O(0,0)\).
✅ Final Answer
(i) x-axis and y-axis; (ii) quadrants; (iii) origin \(O(0,0)\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
(i) x-અક્ષ અને y-અક્ષ; (ii) ચતુર્થાંશો; (iii) મૂળબિંદુ \(O(0,0)\).
📋 Given
See Fig. 3.14 and answer:
(i) coordinates of \(B\); (ii) coordinates of \(C\); (iii) point identified by \((-3,-5)\); (iv) point identified by \((2,-4)\); (v) abscissa of \(D\); (vi) ordinate of \(H\); (vii) coordinates of \(L\); (viii) coordinates of \(M\).
🎯 To Find
Complete the required proof or calculation.
📘 Formula / Concept Used
Read the abscissa first from the x-axis and then the ordinate from the y-axis.
✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. (i) \(B=(-5,2)\).
  2. (ii) \(C=(5,-5)\).
  3. (iii) \((-3,-5)\) represents point \(E\).
  4. (iv) \((2,-4)\) represents point \(G\).
  5. (v) The abscissa of \(D\) is \(6\).
  6. (vi) The ordinate of \(H\) is \(-3\).
  7. (vii) \(L=(0,5)\).
  8. (viii) \(M=(-3,0)\).
✅ Final Answer
\(\boxed{B(-5,2),\;C(5,-5),\;E(-3,-5),\;G(2,-4),\;x_D=6,\;y_H=-3,\;L(0,5),\;M(-3,0)}\).
🌐 Gujarati Translation — Final Answer
અંતિમ જવાબ ઉપર દર્શાવેલા ક્રમમાં છે.
