✅ Answer

Wavelength \(\lambda\): distance between successive compressions or rarefactions.
Frequency \(f\): oscillations per second.
Time period \(T\): time for one oscillation, \(T=1/f\).
Amplitude: maximum displacement/pressure variation from mean position.

✅ Final Answer

Wavelength \(\lambda\): distance between successive compressions or rarefactions. Frequency \(f\): oscillations per second. Time period \(T\): time for one oscillation, \(T=1/f\). Amplitude: maximum displacement/pressure variation from mean position.
