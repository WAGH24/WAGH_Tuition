In-text Question Set 14 question(s)
📋 Given

Reactants: 5.3 g sodium carbonate + 6.0 g acetic acid. Products: 2.2 g CO₂ + 0.9 g H₂O + 8.2 g sodium acetate.

📘 Formula Used

\(\text{Total mass of reactants}=\text{Total mass of products}\)

✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Mass of reactants \(=5.3+6.0=11.3\,g\).
  2. Mass of products \(=2.2+0.9+8.2=11.3\,g\).
  3. Both totals are equal, so mass is conserved.
✅ Final Answer

The observations obey the law of conservation of mass.

🌐 Gujarati Translation

📋 Given

Mass ratio H:O = 1:8; hydrogen = 3 g.

📘 Formula Used

\(m_O=8\,m_H\)

✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. For 1 g hydrogen, oxygen required = 8 g.
  2. For 3 g hydrogen, oxygen required \(=3\times8=24\,g\).
✅ Final Answer

24 g of oxygen is required.

✅ Answer

✅ Final Answer

📋 Given

Total mass = 0.24 g; B = 0.096 g; O = 0.144 g.

📘 Formula Used

\(\%\text{ element}=\dfrac{\text{mass of element}}{\text{mass of compound}}\times100\)

✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Boron: \(0.096/0.24\times100=40\%\).
  2. Oxygen: \(0.144/0.24\times100=60\%\).
✅ Final Answer

Boron = 40%; oxygen = 60%.
