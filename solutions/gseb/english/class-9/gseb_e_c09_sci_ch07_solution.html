✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. \(140/40=3.5\) rounds.
  2. After 3 complete rounds the farmer is at the start.
  3. Another half round places him at the diagonally opposite corner.
  4. Displacement = diagonal \(=\sqrt{10^2+10^2}=10\sqrt2\,m\).
✅ Final Answer

Magnitude of displacement = \(10\sqrt2\,m\approx14.14\,m\).

📋 Given

Diameter = 200 m; one round = 40 s; total time = 140 s.

📘 Formula Used

\(C=\pi d\), \(n=t/40\)

✏️ Step-by-Step Solution
  1. Number of rounds \(=140/40=3.5\).
  2. Using \(\pi=22/7\), circumference \(=\pi\times200\,m\).
  3. Distance \(=3.5\times\pi\times200=2200\,m\).
  4. After 3.5 rounds the athlete is at the opposite point, so displacement = diameter = 200 m.
✅ Final Answer

Distance = 2200 m; displacement = 200 m.
