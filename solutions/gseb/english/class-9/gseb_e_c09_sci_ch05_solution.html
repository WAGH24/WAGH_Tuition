🌐 Gujarati Translation

✅ Answer

(i)

Sugar and salt make the solution inside B and C concentrated. Water enters through living potato cells by osmosis and collects in the hollows.

(ii)

A is the control. It shows that without a solute concentration difference, water does not collect.

(iii)

A has no solute, so no osmotic gradient exists. D is boiled; boiling destroys cell membranes, so the selectively permeable pathway needed for osmosis is lost.

✅ Final Answer

(i) Sugar and salt make the solution inside B and C concentrated. Water enters through living potato cells by osmosis and collects in the hollows. (ii) A is the control. It shows that without a solute concentration difference, water does not collect. (iii) A has no solute, so no osmotic gradient exists. D is boiled; boiling destroys cell membranes, so the selectively permeable pathway needed for osmosis is lost.
