Session 1: Sustainable Development

(a) 18
(b) 17
(c) 15
(d) 20
✅ Correct Answer: (b) 17
🧠 Explanation: The United Nations adopted 17 Sustainable Development Goals.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સંયુક્ત રાષ્ટ્રે 17 ટકાઉ વિકાસ લક્ષ્યો સ્વીકાર્યા છે.
(a) Taking care of future generations
(b) Taking care of only ourselves
(c) Taking care of ourselves and future generations
(d) Well-being of all
✅ Correct Answer: (c)
🧠 Explanation: Sustainable development meets present needs without reducing the ability of future generations to meet theirs.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ટકાઉ વિકાસ વર્તમાન અને ભાવિ પેઢીની જરૂરિયાતોનું ધ્યાન રાખે છે.
(a) United Nations
(b) League of Nations
(c) UNICEF
(d) World Health Organization
✅ Correct Answer: (a) United Nations
🧠 Explanation: The SDGs were created and adopted through the United Nations.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: SDGs સંયુક્ત રાષ્ટ્ર દ્વારા બનાવવામાં આવ્યા.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Sustainable development is development that improves present well-being while conserving natural, social and economic resources so that future generations can also meet their needs. It balances economic growth, social inclusion and environmental protection.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ટકાઉ વિકાસ વર્તમાન જરૂરિયાતો પૂરી કરતાં ભાવિ પેઢીના સંસાધનોનું સંરક્ષણ કરે છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: The goals provide a common global plan to reduce poverty and inequality, improve health and education, protect the planet, promote peace and build sustainable prosperity by 2030. They help governments, organisations and citizens coordinate and measure progress.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: 17 લક્ષ્યો ગરીબી, અસમાનતા, આરોગ્ય, શિક્ષણ, પર્યાવરણ, શાંતિ અને સમૃદ્ધિ માટે વૈશ્વિક સંયુક્ત યોજના આપે છે.

Session 2: Our Role in Sustainable Development

(a) Solar energy
(b) Wood
(c) Coal
(d) Petrol
✅ Correct Answer: (a) Solar energy

(a) Clean Water and Sanitation
(b) Gender Equality
(c) Population
(d) Reduced Inequalities
✅ Correct Answer: (c) Population
🧠 Explanation: There is no SDG named “Population”; the other three are official goal themes.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: “Population” નામનું અલગ SDG નથી.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Reduce unnecessary consumption, reuse and repair items, recycle waste, save water and electricity, use public transport or walk, avoid single-use plastic, choose durable local products, segregate waste, protect trees and biodiversity, and prefer renewable energy where possible.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સંસાધનો બચાવવા ઘટાડો, પુનઃઉપયોગ, મરામત, રિસાયકલ, પાણી-વીજળી બચત, કચરા વિભાજન અને નવીનીકરણીય ઊર્જાનો ઉપયોગ કરો.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Education builds knowledge, values and skills needed to understand environmental and social problems, make responsible choices, use resources efficiently, adopt green practices, participate in communities and create sustainable technologies and livelihoods. Educated citizens can demand accountability and support long-term solutions.
