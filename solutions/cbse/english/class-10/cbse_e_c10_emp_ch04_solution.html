Session 1: Entrepreneurship and Society

✅ Answer: True
🧠 Explanation: Entrepreneurs establish and expand enterprises, which creates direct and indirect employment.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સાચું—ઉદ્યોગસાહસિકો રોજગાર સર્જે છે.
✅ Answer: False
🧠 Explanation: Competition usually improves choice and can reduce prices or improve value.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ખોટું—સ્પર્ધાથી સામાન્ય રીતે કિંમત ઘટે અથવા મૂલ્ય સુધરે છે.
✅ Answer: True
🧠 Explanation: Opportunity recognition and solving customer needs are central entrepreneurial activities.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સાચું—ઉદ્યોગસાહસિક બજારની જરૂરિયાત ઓળખીને ઉત્પાદન/સેવા બનાવે છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Entrepreneurs create jobs, satisfy needs through products and services, introduce innovation, increase competition, support economic growth, pay taxes, use local resources, improve living standards and may contribute to social and community development.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ઉદ્યોગસાહસિક રોજગાર, નવીનતા, સ્પર્ધા, આર્થિક વૃદ્ધિ, કર આવક અને જીવનસ્તર સુધારે છે.

Session 2: Qualities and Functions of an Entrepreneur

(a) Hardworking
(b) Confident
(c) Patient
(d) Trying new ideas
✅ Correct Answer: (c) Patient
🧠 Explanation: Remaining calm and listening despite provocation shows patience.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: રવિ ધીરજ દર્શાવે છે.
(a) Takes responsibility for mistakes
(b) Thinks before making a decision
(c) Does not give up
(d) Is creative
✅ Correct Answer: (a)
🧠 Explanation: She accepts the result of her decision and takes responsibility.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: તે પોતાની ભૂલની જવાબદારી સ્વીકારે છે.
(a) Creates a new product
(b) Manages the business
(c) Takes risk
✅ Correct Answer: (b) Manages the business
🧠 Explanation: Paying employees on schedule is part of organising and managing business operations.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: કર્મચારીઓને સમયસર પગાર આપવો વ્યવસાય સંચાલન છે.
(a) Makes decisions
(b) Divides income
(c) Takes risk
✅ Correct Answer: (a) Makes decisions
🧠 Explanation: She compares alternatives and chooses a more economical supplier.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: મેરી નિર્ણય લેવાની કામગીરી કરે છે.
(a) Creates a new product
(b) Divides income
(c) Manages the business
✅ Correct Answer: (c) Manages the business
🧠 Explanation: Monitoring and guiding employees are management functions.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: કર્મચારીઓનું કામ તપાસવું વ્યવસાય સંચાલન છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: A strong answer is decision-making because every other function depends on choosing what product to offer, whom to serve, how to use money, whom to employ and how to respond to risk. For example, a food entrepreneur decides the menu, price, supplier and location. Good decisions help the enterprise survive and grow.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: નિર્ણય લેવું અત્યંત મહત્વપૂર્ણ કાર્ય છે, કારણ કે ઉત્પાદન, ભાવ, પુરવઠો, કર્મચારી અને જોખમ અંગે યોગ્ય પસંદગી વ્યવસાયને ટકાવે છે.

Session 3: Myths about Entrepreneurship

✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: A misconception is a false or incomplete belief accepted as true, while reality is what evidence and actual experience show. Example: “Entrepreneurs are born, not made” is a misconception; in reality, entrepreneurial skills can be learnt and improved through practice, training and experience.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ગેરમાન્યતા ખોટી માન્યતા છે; હકીકત પુરાવા આધારિત સત્ય છે. “ઉદ્યોગસાહસિક જન્મે છે” ગેરમાન્યતા છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Ramu and Shamu: (c) A person needs a big business to be called an entrepreneur. Anna waiting for more money: the misconception is that a person needs a large amount of money to start. Gauri among many tailors: (a) Every business idea needs to be unique or special. The remaining general misconception is (b) Entrepreneurs are born, not made.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: રામુ-શામુ: મોટો વ્યવસાય જરૂરી; અન્ના: શરૂઆત માટે ઘણું મૂડી જરૂરી; ગૌરી: વિચાર અનન્ય હોવો જરૂરી—આ બધું ગેરમાન્યતા છે.

Session 4: Entrepreneurship as a Career Option

✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Cooking in a restaurant as an employee is wage employment. Owning a clothing business is self-employment. Running one’s own dosa stall is self-employment.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: રેસ્ટોરન્ટમાં નોકરી કરીને રસોઈ—વેતન રોજગાર; કપડાંનો વ્યવસાય અને પોતાનો ઢોસા સ્ટોલ—સ્વરોજગાર.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Surabhi opens five painting stores across India—Grow. Jacob adds morning batches to compete with many coaching classes—Survive. Salma starts her clothing line on the Internet—Enter.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સુરભિ—Grow; જેકબ—Survive; સલમા—Enter.
