Session 1: Methods of Communication

(a) Channel
(b) Receiver
(c) Sender
(d) Time
✅ Correct Answer: (d) Time
🧠 Explanation: The communication cycle includes sender, message, channel, receiver and feedback. Time is not listed as an element.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સમય સંચાર ચક્રનું તત્ત્વ નથી.
(a) e-mail
(b) Poster
(c) Newsletter
(d) Blog
✅ Correct Answer: (a) e-mail
🧠 Explanation: An e-mail is direct, formal, quick and creates a written record of the leave request.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: રજા માટે ઔપચારિક ઈ-મેલ યોગ્ય માધ્યમ છે.
(a) Gestures
(b) Speaking
(c) Reading
(d) Writing
✅ Correct Answer: (a), (b) and (d)
🧠 Explanation: A sender can encode a message through gestures, speech or writing. Reading is mainly a receiving activity.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: મોકલનાર હાવભાવ, બોલવું અને લખવું દ્વારા સંદેશ મોકલી શકે છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: A suitable chart should include face-to-face communication, e-mail, notices/posters, business meetings, phone calls, messages, social networks, newsletters and blogs. Compare them by audience, speed, cost, formality and whether immediate feedback is possible.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ચાર્ટમાં સામસામે સંચાર, ઈ-મેલ, નોટિસ/પોસ્ટર, બેઠક, ફોન, સંદેશા, સામાજિક માધ્યમ, ન્યૂઝલેટર અને બ્લોગનો સમાવેશ કરો.

Session 2: Verbal Communication

(a) Newspapers
(b) Letters
(c) Phone call
(d) e-mail
✅ Correct Answer: (c) Phone call
🧠 Explanation: A phone call uses spoken words and listening, so it is oral communication.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ફોન કૉલ મૌખિક સંચારનું ઉદાહરણ છે.
(a) Acronyms
(b) Simple
(c) Technical
(d) Jargons
✅ Correct Answer: (b) Simple
🧠 Explanation: Simple and familiar words improve clarity and reduce misunderstanding.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: મૌખિક સંચારમાં સરળ શબ્દો વાપરવા જોઈએ.
(a) To communicate with many people at the same time
(b) To share documents and files
(c) To talk to each other in real-time
(d) To keep a record of communication
✅ Correct Answer: (a), (b) and (d)
🧠 Explanation: E-mail supports multiple recipients, attachments and a written record. It is not normally real-time conversation.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ઈ-મેલનો ઉપયોગ ઘણા લોકોને સંદેશ મોકલવા, દસ્તાવેજ શેર કરવા અને નોંધ રાખવા માટે થાય છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: The main types are: interpersonal communication, such as a conversation between two friends; written communication, such as letters, reports, SMS and e-mail; small-group communication, such as team meetings and press conferences; and public communication, such as speeches and election campaigns.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: મૌખિક સંચારના પ્રકારોમાં વ્યક્તિગત, લખિત, નાના જૂથનો અને જાહેર સંચારનો સમાવેશ થાય છે.

Session 3: Non-verbal Communication

(a) Frowning while concentrating
(b) Maintaining eye contact
(c) Smiling continuously
(d) Rolling up your eyes
✅ Correct Answer: (b) Maintaining eye contact
🧠 Explanation: Appropriate eye contact shows attention and confidence. Continuous smiling or eye-rolling may look unnatural or disrespectful.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: યોગ્ય આંખનો સંપર્ક સકારાત્મક અભિવ્યક્તિ છે.
(a) Pride
(b) Professionalism
(c) Confidence
(d) Humility
✅ Correct Answer: (b) Professionalism and (c) Confidence
🧠 Explanation: An upright posture generally communicates confidence, attentiveness and professionalism.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સીધી શરીરભંગિમા આત્મવિશ્વાસ અને વ્યાવસાયિકતા દર્શાવે છે.
(a) Keeping hands in pockets while talking
(b) Talking at moderate speed
(c) Sitting straight
(d) Tilting the head slightly to listen
✅ Correct Answer: (a) Keeping hands in pockets while talking
🧠 Explanation: Keeping hands in pockets can appear casual, closed or inattentive in formal communication.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: વાત કરતી વખતે હાથ ખિસ્સામાં રાખવું યોગ્ય નથી.
(a) 50% of our communication is non-verbal
(b) 20% is through body movements, face and arms
(c) 5% is through voice, tone and pauses
(d) 7% is through words
✅ Correct Answer: (d) 7% is through words
🧠 Explanation: The textbook states that 55% is through body movement, 38% through voice and tone, and 7% through words.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: પાઠ્યપુસ્તક મુજબ 7% સંચાર શબ્દો દ્વારા થાય છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Bad examples are laughing during formal communication, scratching the head, yawning while listening, biting nails, clenching jaws, looking away while someone speaks and an intense stare. Positive examples include appropriate smiling, nodding, standing or sitting straight, maintaining eye contact and a firm handshake.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ખરાબ હાવભાવમાં ઔપચારિક વાતચીતમાં હસવું, માથું ખંજવાળવું, જંભાઈ લેવી, નખ ચાવવું, જડબાં ભીંસવું, નજર ફેરવવી અને ઘૂરવું આવે છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Examples: No Entry—entry prohibited, seen at restricted gates; No Parking—parking prohibited, seen on roads; Under Construction—work in progress, seen near building sites; No Mobile Phone—phone use prohibited, seen in hospitals or examination halls; CCTV Surveillance—the area is monitored, seen in shops, banks and offices.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ઉદાહરણ તરીકે પ્રવેશ નિષેધ, પાર્કિંગ નિષેધ, બાંધકામ ચાલુ, મોબાઇલ પ્રતિબંધ અને CCTV દેખરેખના ચિહ્નો દોરી શકાય.

Session 4: Communication Cycle and Importance of Feedback

(a) Excellent, your work has improved.
(b) I noticed your dedication towards the project.
(c) You are always doing it the wrong way.
(d) All of the above
✅ Correct Answer: (a) and (b)
🧠 Explanation: Options (a) and (b) recognise improvement and effort. Option (c) is negative and unspecific.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: (a) અને (b) સકારાત્મક પ્રતિસાદ છે.
(a) I hate to tell you this but your drawing skills are poor.
(b) You can surely improve your drawing.
(c) This is a good drawing but you can do better.
(d) None of the above
✅ Correct Answer: (a)
🧠 Explanation: Option (a) is harsh and discouraging. Options (b) and (c) are constructive.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: વિકલ્પ (a) નકારાત્મક પ્રતિસાદ છે.
(a) Detailed and time consuming
(b) Direct and honest
(c) Specific
(d) Opinion-based
✅ Correct Answer: (b) and (c)
🧠 Explanation: Good feedback should be direct, honest, specific, timely, polite and helpful.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સારો પ્રતિસાદ સીધો, પ્રામાણિક અને ચોક્કસ હોવો જોઈએ.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Feedback is the receiver’s response or acknowledgement that completes the communication cycle. A suitable response is: “Radha, your section was submitted after the agreed deadline, so the complete report was delayed and both of us received a warning. Please prepare a small schedule for the remaining tasks and tell me early if you need help. Let us review the next report one day before submission.”
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: પ્રતિસાદ સંદેશનો જવાબ છે. રાધાને વિલંબનું ચોક્કસ કારણ સમજાવી સમયપત્રક બનાવવા અને સમયસર મદદ માંગવા વિનમ્ર સૂચન આપવું જોઈએ.

Session 5: Barriers to Effective Communication

(a) Linguistic barrier
(b) Interpersonal barrier
(c) Financial barrier
(d) Organisational barrier
✅ Correct Answer: (c) Financial barrier
🧠 Explanation: The textbook lists physical, linguistic, interpersonal, organisational and cultural barriers.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: આર્થિક અવરોધ સામાન્ય સંચાર અવરોધ તરીકે દર્શાવાયો નથી.
(a) Respecting each other’s differences
(b) Using a translator
(c) Not communicating at all
(d) Using your own language for comfort
✅ Correct Answer: (a) and (b)
🧠 Explanation: Respect and translation can reduce cultural and linguistic barriers. Avoiding communication does not solve the problem.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: એકબીજાના ભેદનો માન રાખવો અને અનુવાદકનો ઉપયોગ કરવો અવરોધ દૂર કરવાની રીતો છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Possible barriers include unfamiliar language and accent, different customs and gestures, lack of local knowledge, physical distance, fear or hesitation, stereotypes, unfamiliar organisational rules and limited access to suitable communication channels. These can be reduced through simple language, visuals, translation, respectful listening and face-to-face interaction.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: નવી જગ્યાએ ભાષા, ઉચ્ચાર, સંસ્કૃતિ, અજાણ નિયમો, સંકોચ અને અંતર જેવા અવરોધ આવી શકે છે.

Session 6: Writing Skills — Parts of Speech

(a) Radha has a red dress.
(b) I can speak French.
(c) The Girl on the Train is a best-seller.
(d) Abdul can swim fast.
✅ Correct Answer: (a) Radha has a red dress.
🧠 Explanation: “Red” describes the noun “dress”, so it is an adjective.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: “Red” શબ્દ dress નામનું વર્ણન કરે છે, તેથી તે વિશેષણ છે.
(a) Ravi and i are going to the movies.
(b) Salim is visiting India in july.
(c) The Tiger is a strong animal.
(d) She is arriving on Monday.
✅ Correct Answer: (d) She is arriving on Monday.
🧠 Explanation: The pronoun I, proper names, months and days require capital letters. Option (d) follows the rule correctly.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: વિકલ્પ (d) માં મોટા અક્ષરનો યોગ્ય ઉપયોગ થયો છે.
(a) When is the party.
(b) I had bread omelette and a Banana for breakfast.
(c) I am so excited about my first foreign trip!
(d) This is Abdul’s notebook.
✅ Correct Answer: (c) and (d)
🧠 Explanation: Option (c) correctly uses an exclamation mark and option (d) correctly uses an apostrophe and full stop.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: વિકલ્પ (c) અને (d) માં વિરામચિહ્નો યોગ્ય છે.
(a) Divya drinks milk every day.
(b) Sanjay gifted me a new pen.
(c) I opened the door lock.
(d) Sita is 5-feet tall.
✅ Correct Answer: (a)
🧠 Explanation: “Every day” tells how often Divya drinks milk, so it functions adverbially.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: “Every day” ક્રિયા કેટલી વાર થાય છે તે બતાવે છે, તેથી તે ક્રિયાવિશેષણ છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Suitable completed sentences are: (a) The girl is swimming. (b) The children are playing. (c) The students are writing. (d) Rahim is driving the car. (e) Ms Sen is teaching. (f) The cat is eating.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: યોગ્ય જવાબો: છોકરી તરતી છે; બાળકો રમે છે; વિદ્યાર્થીઓ લખે છે; રહીમ કાર ચલાવે છે; મિસ સેન ભણાવે છે; બિલાડી ખાય છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Conjunctions: because, and, since, although, or. Prepositions: over, under, in, at, up, on, beside.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: સમુચ્ચયબોધક: because, and, since, although, or. નામયોગી: over, under, in, at, up, on, beside.

Session 7: Writing Skills — Sentences

(a) Object: a tree; Verb: crashed; Subject: the car
(b) Object: the car; Verb: crashed; Subject: a tree
(c) Object: crashed; Verb: the tree; Subject: the car
(d) Object: crashed; Verb: the car; Subject: the tree
✅ Correct Answer: (a)
🧠 Explanation: The car performs the action, “crashed” is the verb and “a tree” receives the action.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: વિષય “the car”, ક્રિયા “crashed” અને કર્મ “a tree” છે.
(a) The band
(b) played
(c) music
(d) audience
✅ Correct Answer: (d) audience
🧠 Explanation: Music is the direct object; the audience is the recipient, so it is the indirect object.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: “Audience” પરોક્ષ કર્મ છે.
(a) Switch off the fan.
(b) Sheila has gone to the market.
(c) Where are my pen colours?
(d) Oh no! I missed my flight.
✅ Correct Answer: (a)
🧠 Explanation: It gives a command, so it is imperative.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: “Switch off the fan.” આજ્ઞાર્થ વાક્ય છે.
(a) A movie is being watched by them.
(b) The car was repaired by Raju.
(c) He is reading a book.
(d) The thief was being chased by a policeman.
✅ Correct Answer: (c)
🧠 Explanation: The subject “He” performs the action “is reading”, so the sentence is active.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: વિકલ્પ (c) કર્તરી વાચ્યમાં છે.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Statements: “The library opens at nine.” “Riya enjoys science.” Questions: “Where is your notebook?” “Have you finished the task?” Exclamatory: “What a beautiful painting!” “Hurray! We won the match!” Orders: “Please close the door.” “Submit the form today.”
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: દરેક પ્રકારના બે યોગ્ય ઉદાહરણો લખવા: નિવેદન, પ્રશ્ન, ઉદ્ગાર અને આજ્ઞા.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Sample: “My favourite dish is vegetable pulao. It is colourful, nutritious and easy to eat. Rice, vegetables and mild spices give it a pleasant flavour. I especially enjoy it with curd. My family often prepares it on weekends. Vegetable pulao is also a balanced meal when it contains many vegetables. It gives energy from rice and vitamins from vegetables. I like helping to wash and cut the vegetables. The aroma while it cooks makes everyone hungry. For these reasons, vegetable pulao is my favourite dish.”
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: નમૂનામાં મનપસંદ ભોજનનું નામ, સ્વાદ, સામગ્રી, પૌષ્ટિકતા અને વ્યક્તિગત પસંદગી બે સુવ્યવસ્થિત પરિચ્છેદમાં લખવી.
✅ Answer:
🧠 Explanation: Example pair: Active—“The chef prepared the meal.” Passive—“The meal was prepared by the chef.” In the active sentence, “the chef” is the subject, “prepared” is the verb and “the meal” is the object.
🌐 Gujarati Final Answer: ઉદાહરણ: કર્તરી—The chef prepared the meal. કર્મણી—The meal was prepared by the chef.
