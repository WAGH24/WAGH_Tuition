Chapter 3: The Mystery of Food

Board: CBSEClass: 5Subject: ScienceMedium: English
For Class 5 learners: This page keeps only textbook in-text questions and chapter-end questions. Activity tasks are intentionally skipped. Answers use simple scientific language, clear reasoning and child-friendly visuals.

🔑 Key Words to Remember

MicrobesVery tiny living things; many need a microscope to be seen.
MouldA visible growth formed by colonies of microbes on food.
Food spoilageUnwanted changes that make food smell, look or taste bad or unsafe.
PreservationMethods used to keep food from spoiling for longer.
DehydrationRemoving moisture from food, often by drying.
RefrigerationKeeping food cold to slow the growth of microbes.
FermentationA process in which helpful microbes bring changes in food.
SalivaLiquid in the mouth that mixes with food and helps digestion.
Oral hygieneKeeping the mouth and teeth clean and healthy.
ChokingWhen food blocks the passage used for breathing.

PART I — In-text Questions

✅ Answer
Model answer: An upset stomach can happen after eating spoiled or unsuitable food. Spoiled food may cause stomach pain, vomiting or indigestion. Rest, safe fluids and suitable home care may help, but if the problem is severe an adult should consult a doctor.
The textbook asks students to share their own experience, so personal answers may differ.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
બગડેલું અથવા અનુકૂળ ન હોય તેવું ખોરાક ખાવાથી પેટ ખરાબ થઈ શકે. પેટદર્દ, ઉલટી અથવા અપચો થઈ શકે. ગંભીર તકલીફમાં વડીલની મદદથી ડૉક્ટરની સલાહ લેવી.
✅ Answer
  1. Microbes are present around us.
  2. They grow well when moisture, air and a suitable temperature are available.
  3. As they grow, they can change the smell, taste and appearance of food.
Final Answer: Food spoils mainly because microbes grow on it and cause changes.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
ખોરાક પર સૂક્ષ્મજીવો વધે છે અને તેની ગંધ, સ્વાદ તથા દેખાવમાં ફેરફાર કરે છે, તેથી ખોરાક બગડે છે.
✅ Answer
Possible examples: chillies, papad, some herbs or spices, and certain fruits/vegetables that families traditionally dry.
Reason: Drying removes moisture, so microbes cannot grow easily.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
મરચાં, પાપડ, કેટલીક જડીબુટ્ટી/મસાલા અથવા કેટલાક ફળ-શાક સુકવી સાચવી શકાય છે. સુકવવાથી ભેજ ઓછો થાય છે.
✅ Answer
  1. Oil forms a layer that keeps much of the air away from the pickle.
  2. Without that protection, microbes can grow more easily.
Final Answer: The pickle may spoil faster if oil is not added properly.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
તેલ હવાને દૂર રાખવામાં મદદ કરે છે. તેલ ન હોય તો સૂક્ષ્મજીવો ઝડપથી વધી શકે અને અથાણું વહેલું બગડી શકે.
✅ Answer
  1. Butter contains more moisture than ghee.
  2. When butter is heated to make ghee, water separates and evaporates.
  3. With much less moisture, ghee keeps for longer than butter.
Final Answer: Butter is kept cold because its moisture allows faster spoilage; ghee has much less water after heating.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
માખણમાં ઘી કરતાં વધુ ભેજ હોય છે. ઘી બનાવતી વખતે પાણી અલગ થઈ બાષ્પીભવન થાય છે, તેથી ઘી વધુ સમય ટકે છે.
✅ Answer
  1. Idli batter is prepared and kept for some time before cooking.
  2. Microbes present around us help bring changes in the batter.
  3. The batter rises and becomes fluffy.
Final Answer: Helpful microbes make the idli batter rise and become fluffy.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
ઇડલીનું ખીરું થોડો સમય રાખતાં ઉપયોગી સૂક્ષ્મજીવો તેમાં ફેરફાર કરે છે અને ખીરું ફૂલીને નરમ બને છે.
✅ Answer
Personal-response question: Write only what actually happened in your family. The chapter mentions curd or buttermilk as foods sometimes given for an upset stomach because they contain helpful microbes.
If symptoms are severe, the textbook says a doctor may need to be consulted.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
આ વ્યક્તિગત જવાબ છે. તમારા ઘરે આપવામાં આવેલ સાચો ઘરગથ્થુ ઉપાય લખો. પાઠમાં દહીં/છાશનો ઉલ્લેખ છે. ગંભીર તકલીફમાં ડૉક્ટરની સલાહ લેવી.
✅ Answer
Region-specific question: Answers vary. Write three fruits or vegetables that are naturally common in particular seasons where you live.
The textbook gives examples such as amla in winter and jamun in summer.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
આ વિસ્તાર આધારિત પ્રશ્ન છે. તમારા વિસ્તારમાં ખાસ ઋતુમાં મળતા ત્રણ ફળ અથવા શાકના નામ લખો. પાઠમાં શિયાળામાં આમળા અને ઉનાળામાં જાંબુનું ઉદાહરણ છે.
✅ Answer
  1. Take small mouthfuls of food.
  2. Chew food properly before swallowing.
  3. Eat slowly instead of gulping food.
  4. Avoid talking or laughing with food in your mouth.
Final Answer: Eat slowly, take small bites and chew properly to reduce the risk of choking.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
નાના કોળિયા લો, ધીમે ખાઓ, સારી રીતે ચાવો અને મોઢામાં ખોરાક હોય ત્યારે વાત કે હાસ્ય ટાળો.

PART II — Chapter End Questions

“Let us reflect” questions are kept in their original order.

✅ Answer
  1. Microbes need suitable conditions such as moisture, air and temperature to grow.
  2. Foods with more moisture or favourable conditions may spoil faster.
  3. Preservation methods such as drying, oiling, airtight storage and refrigeration make conditions less suitable for microbes.
Final Answer: Foods last for different lengths of time because their conditions for microbial growth and their preservation methods are different.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
ભેજ, હવા અને તાપમાન જેવી પરિસ્થિતિઓ તથા સાચવણીની પદ્ધતિઓ અલગ હોવાથી કેટલાક ખોરાક ઝડપથી બગડે છે અને કેટલાક લાંબા સમય ટકે છે.
✅ Answer
Sample plan:
Food itemHow to keep it safe
Dry snacksKeep in a clean airtight container.
Roasted grains / similar dry foodKeep dry and covered.
Whole fruitCarry fresh, clean fruit and avoid crushing it.
Khakhra / other dry flatbreadKeep dry in a sealed box.
Another suitable non-spoiling food chosen at homePack cleanly and follow adult guidance.
Choose foods suitable for travel and follow food-safety guidance from adults.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
બે દિવસની સફર માટે સુકા અને સરળતાથી ન બગડતા ખોરાક પસંદ કરો, સ્વચ્છ અને હવામાંથી બંધ ડબ્બામાં રાખો અને વડીલોની સલાહ અનુસરો.
✅ Answer
  1. Much more food would spoil before it could be eaten.
  2. It would be difficult to store seasonal foods for later use.
  3. People travelling or living far from farms and markets would have fewer food choices.
  4. Food waste would increase.
Final Answer: Without preservation, food would spoil faster, waste would rise and storing food for later would become much harder.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
સાચવણી ન હોય તો ખોરાક ઝડપથી બગડે, બગાડ વધે અને ઋતુ બહાર અથવા લાંબા સમય માટે ખોરાક રાખવો મુશ્કેલ બને.
✅ Answer
Item → ProductMethod
Roti → KhakhraDehydration / removal of moisture by heating
Papad → Dried papadsDrying in the sun
Mango → Aam papadDrying / dehydration
Lemon → Lemon picklePickling, using oil/salt or related traditional preservation

Key idea: Different preservation methods reduce conditions that help food spoil.
