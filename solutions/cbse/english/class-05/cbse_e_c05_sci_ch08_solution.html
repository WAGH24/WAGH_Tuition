Chapter 8: Clothes — How Things are Made

Board: CBSEClass: 5Subject: ScienceMedium: EnglishChapter: Clothes — How Things are Made
Class 5 learning page: Activities are skipped. Only textbook in-text questions and chapter-end questions are included. Answers are short, clear, complete and connected to the chapter’s learning goals.

🔑 Key Words to Remember

WeavingCrossing threads or strips over and under each other to make fabric or other woven objects.
LoomA tool or machine used for weaving cloth.
HandloomCloth woven by hand using a loom.
SpinningTwisting fibres together to make thread or yarn.
FibreA thin, hair-like strand used to make yarn or thread.
Natural fibreFibre obtained from plants or animals, such as cotton, jute, wool or silk.
Synthetic fibreHuman-made fibre, such as nylon or polyester.
Running stitchA simple stitch made by passing a needle up and down through cloth.

PART I — In-text Questions

✅ Answer
The picture shows weaving-like patterns in nature. Birds, spiders and other living things can make nests, webs or structures using fibres and threads.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
ચિત્રમાં પ્રકૃતિમાં જોવા મળતી વણાટ જેવી રચનાઓ દેખાય છે.
✅ Answer
The baya weaverbird is one such natural weaver. The male baya weaver carefully weaves grass strands over and under to make a hanging nest.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
બાયા વીવર પક્ષી પ્રકૃતિનો વણકર છે; તે ઘાસના તંતુઓ વણીને લટકતું માળું બનાવે છે.
✅ Answer
Examples include bamboo baskets, cane chairs, jute bags, mats made from grass or palm leaves, and cotton or silk cloth.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
બાંસની ટોપલી, કેન ખુરશી, જૂટની થેલી, ઘાસ/પાનની ચટાઈ અને કપાસ કે રેશમનું કાપડ ઉદાહરણ છે.
✅ Answer
Examples may include cloth bags, curtains, uniforms, mats or fabric covers. Their threads are crossed in an over-under pattern.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
ક્લાસરૂમમાં કપડાની થેલી, પડદા, યુનિફોર્મ, ચટાઈ અથવા કપડાના કવર વણાયેલા હોઈ શકે છે.
✅ Answer
Threads are made by spinning: fibres such as cotton are pulled and twisted together so they become stronger and form yarn or thread.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
તંતુઓને ખેંચીને અને વાળી ને મજબૂત સૂત બનાવવાની પ્રક્રિયાને કાતવું (spinning) કહે છે.
✅ Answer
Running stitches can be used to join pieces of cloth, mend a small tear, make a simple hem, attach patches or create decorative patterns.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
રનિંગ સ્ટિચથી કાપડ જોડવું, નાનું ફાટેલું ભાગ સુધારવું, પટ્ટી/પેચ લગાવવું અને શણગાર કરવો શક્ય છે.
✅ Answer
The nearby stitches may loosen and the joined cloth may start opening. Each thread helps hold the fabric together, so a broken thread can weaken the stitched area.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
એક દોરો તૂટે તો આસપાસના ટાંકા ઢીલા પડી શકે અને જોડાણ નબળું થઈ શકે.

✅ Answer
Old clothes can often be reused, repaired, donated, repurposed or recycled. This reduces waste and saves the materials and energy needed to make new cloth.

1. Silk moth lays eggs. 2. Eggs hatch into tiny caterpillars. 3. Caterpillars eat mulberry leaves and grow big. 4. Caterpillars spin cocoons around themselves. 5. Adult moth comes out of the cocoon. 6. The cycle begins again.
