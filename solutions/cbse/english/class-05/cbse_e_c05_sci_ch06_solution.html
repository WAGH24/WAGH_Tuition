🔑 Key Words to Remember

IslandLand surrounded by water on all sides.
EndemicNaturally found only in a particular place or region.
Coral reefA living underwater structure built by tiny coral animals and used as habitat by many sea creatures.
MangroveA salt-tolerant tree or shrub that grows in muddy coastal areas.
BiodiversityThe variety of living things in an area.
Root bridgeA bridge formed by guiding and growing living tree roots.
Western GhatsA long mountain range along western India, rich in plants and animals.
Protected areaA place managed to conserve nature and wildlife.
HotspotA region especially rich in unique biodiversity and important for conservation.
Harmony with natureLiving in ways that respect and depend on nature without destroying it.
