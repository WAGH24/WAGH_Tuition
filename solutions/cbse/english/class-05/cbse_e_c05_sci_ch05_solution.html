Chapter 5: Our Vibrant Country

Board: CBSEClass: 5Subject: ScienceMedium: English
For Class 5 learners: This page keeps only textbook in-text questions and chapter-end questions. Activity tasks are intentionally skipped. Answers use simple scientific language, clear reasoning and child-friendly visuals.

🔑 Key Words to Remember

Republic Day26 January, when India celebrates the day its Constitution came into effect.
National FlagIndia’s tricolour flag with the Ashoka Chakra.
ConstitutionThe highest set of principles and laws that guides the country.
DiversityThe presence of many languages, foods, clothes, arts and traditions.
UnityLiving and working together despite differences.
CurrencyMoney used in a country.
MonumentA building or structure important for history, culture or memory.
LanguageA system people use to communicate.
TraditionA custom or practice passed from one generation to another.
Vasudhaiva KutumbakamAn Indian idea meaning “the world is one family”.

✅ Answer
Model answer: Schools commonly hold flag hoisting, sing the National Anthem, present patriotic programmes and remember the freedom struggle.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
સ્વતંત્રતા દિવસે ધ્વજવંદન, રાષ્ટ્રગાન, દેશભક્તિ કાર્યક્રમો અને સ્વાતંત્ર્ય સંગ્રામનું સ્મરણ થાય છે.
✅ Answer
Example: Be punctual; keep the school clean; respect others; walk safely in corridors; use water and electricity carefully. New rule: Every class checks that lights, fans and taps are off before leaving.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
ઉદાહરણ: સમયસર આવવું, સ્વચ્છતા, સૌનો સન્માન, સુરક્ષિત રીતે ચાલવું, પાણી-વીજળી બચાવવી. નવો નિયમ: વર્ગ છોડતાં પહેલાં લાઇટ-પંખા-નળ તપાસવા.
✅ Answer
State-specific answer: You may include local food, dress, language, festivals, music, dance, crafts, buildings or natural features. Use examples from your own state.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
તમારા રાજ્યના ખોરાક, વસ્ત્ર, ભાષા, તહેવાર, સંગીત, નૃત્ય, હસ્તકલા, ઇમારતો અથવા પ્રકૃતિના ઉદાહરણો લખો.
✅ Answer
Answer: Different students may speak different languages or mother tongues. This shows India’s linguistic diversity and how people with different backgrounds learn together.
🌐 ગુજરાતી અનુવાદ
વિદ્યાર્થીઓ જુદી જુદી ભાષા બોલી શકે છે. તે ભારતની ભાષાકીય વિવિધતા અને સાથે શીખવાની એકતા બતાવે છે.
✅ Answer
Examples: tabla, sitar, veena, flute, shehnai, dhol, mridangam and sarangi.
Write instruments you have actually seen or learned about.
